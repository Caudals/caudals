# L-10 · Price book, model guard and estimator — handoff

Package ID / title: L-10 · Price book, model guard and estimator (spec v2 §6.3, §6.4, §6.7, §6.13, §6.16; P-16 backend; G9; D-03, D-09)
Status: DRAFT (in progress on `launch/L-10`)

## Implemented behaviour

- **Price book (migration 088).** `billing.price_book_revision` (draft → scheduled → active → retired, effective dates, FX and multiplier snapshot, `raises_prices`, `notice_sent_at`), `billing.model_rate` (µcr per 1M input/output/cached tokens by provider, model id and prompt-size tier, with the USD list price, FX, routing fee, EUR cost and source), `billing.action_rate` (the six §6.3 meters), `billing.fx_rate` (ECB) and `billing.price_check` (weekly diffs, append-only). Rates change only on drafts; triggers reject edits, deletes and TRUNCATE on scheduled, active and retired revisions. The database enforces the 5-credit grid and the 10-credit floor.
- **Revision in effect.** `billing.price_book_at(t)`: the latest non-draft revision with `effective_at ≤ t`, so a scheduled revision applies exactly on time; the hourly scheduler job only relabels statuses. Revision 1 is in effect from 2026-10-11 (the §6.4 table and meters, ECB 1.1605 USD/EUR). Revision 2 is scheduled for 2027-01-01 with Google's announced Gemini 3.8 Flash increase (195 · 970 credits).
- **Lifecycle (platform admins).** Draft from the revision in effect (list prices recomputed at the latest recorded FX, then per-model changes), schedule (needs every meter and the local rate, follows every other scheduled revision, keeps every current route priced, 30 days' notice for any increase), cancel (before it takes effect), activate (a non-increase comes forward to now). Audited.
- **€0 guard (G9).** Trigger on platform, workspace and evaluation model routes: `MODEL_PRICE_ZERO` when a non-DGX model has a €0 cost price, `MODEL_RATE_MISSING` when it has no credit rate in the revision in effect or a scheduled one, unless the provider revision is an explicit `free_model` (new immutable column; registered OpenRouter `:free` models were flagged by the migration). Settings → AI models: credit rate per route, "€0 price · not for customers" badge, €0 warning and a free-model checkbox in the model dialog. Providers & models: the same badges per model revision and a summary warning. Local stays the default (D-09).
- **Estimator.** `lib/billing/estimate.ts` + `POST /api/evals/v1/billing/estimates`. Exact platform meters; engine tokens per role from 60-day platform-wide aggregates (`billing.estimate_stats`) with the 20-sample fallback to the §6.7 seeds; p50 = n × mean, p90 = n × mean + 1.2816 × sd × √n; largest hold from the p90 token bound; ETA from seconds per question of completed runs. Editors (owner, editor, operator) and CI tokens (`runs:read`), `Idempotency-Key` required, flag `BILLING_ESTIMATES_ENABLED` (`operators` | `true`, default off).
- **Prices over time.** Weekly provider-price check (OpenRouter public models API, no key) and monthly ECB FX in the evals scheduler; `scripts/billing/check-provider-prices.ts` prints the diff on demand.
- **Backtest.** `scripts/billing/backtest-estimates.ts` on read-only production aggregates.

## Results so far

- Formula reproduces every §6.4 credit rate, cost and "50-question run" figure (unit tests).
- Backtest on production (2026-10-12, read-only): 35 of 38 finished runs within ±25 % (92.1 %, target ≥ 80 %): see below.
- Live price check (2026-10-12): ECB 1.1206 USD/EUR on 2026-10-09 is 3.4 % below the book's 1.1605, so every list-priced rate is flagged for recomputation (costs +3.6 %); OpenRouter lists DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at $0.15 · $0.60 and DeepSeek V4 Pro at $0.955 · $1.911 against the book's DeepSeek peak-hour prices.
