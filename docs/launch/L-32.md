## What shipped

- **Answer retention (migration 105 `evals_retention_setting`).** `evals.workspace_retention.observation_days`: 30, 90 or 365 days, or unset. Unset is the default and the behaviour before L-32 (answers are kept). Owners change it in Settings → Data (`GET/PUT /api/evals/v1/workspaces/:id/retention`) through `evals.set_observation_retention()`, which refuses anyone else and writes `workspace.retention_changed` with the before and after values. A shorter period first shows a confirmation with read-only counts (`?preview=`: answers that would go now, answers the pins keep).
- **Purge through the existing retention jobs.** The scheduler (hourly, and the API at once when an owner shortens the period) queues one `evals.retention_job` per run (`object_type` `observation_run`, action `redact`) that has unpinned answers older than the setting; the lifecycle pass (every minute) runs `evals.purge_retained_observations()`. A purged observation loses its messages, tool events and artifact references and gains `purged_at` and the `caudals.evals/retention` extension; it keeps its ids, status, timing and usage metadata and its original `content_hash`, so assessments, findings and counts keep their provenance. One `recovery_control_event` `observation_purged` per answer (replayed by `replayRecoveryControls` after a restore) and one audit `retention.observations_purged` per run. The observation trigger (`evals.guard_observation`, same trigger name) still refuses every other UPDATE and every DELETE. With the flag off, queued purges fail with `data_controls_off` and are re-armed an hour after the flag returns.
- **Retention pins (`evals.observation_retention_pinned`).** Never purged: answers of a run that is still active (queued, running, pausing, paused, cancelling), that has queued judge calls, or that a kept report (not archived, not withdrawn) was built from, and answers a regression case was drawn from. Report snapshots keep their own copy of the answers they show. A run with purged answers cannot be graded or reported again (`RETENTION_EXPIRED`, 409).
- **Export all workspace data (also migration 105).** Owners only (`POST/GET /api/evals/v1/exports/workspace`, `DELETE …/:id`, `GET …/:id/download`). `evals.workspace_export` is a background job the scheduler builds off its main loop (`lib/evals/operations/workspace-export.ts`, ZIP writer `lib/evals/exports/zip.ts`): `README.txt` in the requester's language, `manifest.json` (counts, every file's size and SHA-256), `reports/` (each kept report: `report.<locale>.html`, and per revision `snapshot.json`, `results.csv`, `evidence.cef.jsonl`), `test-sets/` and `runs/` as CEF JSONL (questions, rubrics, every answer including purged ones, every grade), `systems/` and `sources/` indexes (no credentials, no document contents, no deleted items). Limits: 256 MiB uncompressed, 64 MiB ZIP, 50,000 files, 10 minutes (`export_too_large`, `export_timed_out` shown to the owner with the privacy address). Sealed in private storage, streamed with its checksum verified, deleted by the retention jobs (`object_type` `workspace_export`, recovery event `export_deleted`) after 7 days or when the owner deletes it. Workspace deletion cancels open exports and deletes stored ones (new data type only; the deletion workflow is otherwise unchanged). Audit: `workspace.export_requested`, `_completed`, `_failed`, `_downloaded`, `_deleted`.
- **Countersigned DPA (migration 108 `evals_dpa_countersigned`, the L-30 follow-up).** `evals.dpa_countersigned` links one stored PDF (≤ 10 MB, `%PDF-` checked) to a DPA acceptance in `evals.terms_acceptance` (foreign key). Written only by `evals.attach_dpa_countersigned()` and `evals.remove_dpa_countersigned()` (platform operators and administrators; one live copy per acceptance; removal needs a reason, deletes the stored file and keeps the record); a trigger refuses every other change, DELETE and TRUNCATE. RLS: the workspace's owners and platform operations read it; editors, viewers and other workspaces see nothing. Audit: `legal.dpa_countersigned_attached`, `_downloaded`, `_removed`. API: `POST /api/evals/v1/workspaces/:id/dpa/countersigned?acceptanceId=&fileName=` (raw `application/pdf`), `GET/DELETE …/countersigned/:id`. Settings → General's DPA row shows owners "Countersigned copy of version …" with a download link, and operations "Attach countersigned PDF" / "Remove copy".
- **Settings → Data and Danger zone (EN/ES).** New Data tab (every member sees it; owners act). The Danger zone now lists what deletion does: access and share links, credentials, API tokens, provider keys and destinations, runs and reports, stored files, the closed workspace and how to ask for erasure of the remaining records, backups (about a week, re-applied on restore), no recall; with the flag on it points to the export. The help article "How we handle your data" covers retention, export and deletion in both languages.

## Risks and notes

- Retention applies only to answers (observations). Reports keep their own copy of the answers they show, and pinned answers stay until the report is withdrawn or deleted; the setting's help text says so.
- A purged answer keeps its original `content_hash`, which then fingerprints content no longer stored; the `caudals.evals/retention` extension marks it, and the export README explains it.
- The export is built in the scheduler (runtime role, tenant-scoped) rather than the document worker, whose restricted role cannot read answers. One export runs at a time, off the scheduler's main loop.
- ⌘K does not list the Data tab (the palette does not know the flag).
- Platform operators need workspace access (membership or platform admin) to open a customer's Settings and attach a countersigned copy.
