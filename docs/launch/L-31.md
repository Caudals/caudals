## What shipped

- **Written consent (P-23).** On a published report, owners see "Public page" (`components/evals/public-results-consent.tsx`, only with the flag on). They choose a public results page and/or the anonymous sector index, approve the name and a one-sentence scope, pick the index sector (preset from the workspace's L-25 sector), read the versioned consent wording (`2026-10-14`, pending legal review), type their name and role and tick the agreement; nothing is sent without it. The record (`evals.publication_consent`) keeps signer, date, scope, wording version and language; it changes only once, when it is revoked, and is never deleted. Owners withdraw it from the same dialog; a published page goes down in the same transaction. Workspace operators and administrators read; only owners sign (an administrator can record a consent signed outside the app, with a document reference). API `GET/POST/PATCH /api/evals/v1/reports/:id/publication` (404 with the flag off). Its error sentences are in the L-23b API-error gate (`lib/publication` added to its roots).
- **Founder publication.** `scripts/evals/public-results.ts` (list, preview, publish, unpublish, revoke, record-signed, snapshot-sector) prints SQL for psql. `evals.publish_result_page` (platform administrators) needs a standing consent, a published revision, a complete headline and ≥ 10 scored answers, and freezes a projection: name and scope from the consent, test dates, languages, questions, scored answers, correct/partly/incorrect/not scored, strict pass rate and 95% Wilson interval, review level and expert count, scorer version, publication and consent dates. No question, answer, finding, source or system text is copied (tested with marker content).
- **Public results page** `/{locale}/results/<slug>` (`/es/resultados/<slug>`, `components/public/results-page.tsx`), Paper reading layout: who chose to publish, test and publication dates, the share that matched the sources with its interval, the outcome table with ink marks, what was tested, sample, review, methodology link and the limits (one sample on the dates shown; agreement with their own sources, not professional correctness; "not a certification, an audit or a conformity assessment"; Caudals endorses no product; revocable). No badge, seal or score artwork. Rendered per request, so a withdrawal is live on the next request; `noindex` until `PUBLIC_RESULTS_INDEXED`.
- **Anonymous sector index (P-53)** `/{locale}/sector-index/<quarter>` (`/es/indice-sectorial/2026-q4`, `components/public/sector-index-page.tsx`): renders only editions with a recorded legal sign-off and ≥ 10 systems: sector, systems, median share matching the sources, middle half of systems, answers scored, pooled outcome shares; method and limits on the page. Inputs (`evals.sector_index_inputs`, administrators only) are consenting customers and our own snapshots with an assigned sector, one identifier-free row per system (chat host, else target; a customer's consent before a snapshot of the same chat); `lib/publication/sector-index.ts` suppresses sectors under 10 and never shows a minimum or maximum. `scripts/evals/sector-index.ts` reads (READ ONLY), previews and, after the sign-off, records; the database recounts the cohort and pooled answers and refuses under 10, an unended quarter, unknown aggregate keys or a missing sign-off (reference, reviewer, date).
- **Third parties.** Prospect sandboxes (L-21) can never consent or be published (refused in every function, filtered from the public view); snapshots feed only the anonymous aggregates, after an administrator assigns their sector.
- **Paths (decision I-14).** Translated prefixes for dynamic pages (`PREFIX_PATHS` in `lib/i18n/routing.ts`; the proxy rewrites `/es/resultados/…` and redirects the other language's prefix). No removed route returns. Not in the sitemap, `llms.txt` or agent markdown.
- **Docs.** ARCHITECTURE (new section), DESIGN §13 and change log, TOOLS (founder commands), PROGRESS (I-14, §5).

## Risks and follow-ups

- Reputational and legal exposure (spec risk): mitigated by consent records, manual publication, the evidence floor (complete headline, ≥ 10 scored answers), fixed limits on the page, the cohort floor of 10 with no extremes, no identifiers in any index output and a recorded legal sign-off per edition. The wording is pending legal review (I-2).
- A small cohort just above 10 with a narrow sector can still let a well-informed reader guess participants; the lawyer may want a higher floor or coarser figures (one constant, `MIN_COHORT`, and the database CHECK).
- Not built: sitemap/`llms.txt` inclusion once `PUBLIC_RESULTS_INDEXED` is on, an ops UI for publication (the founder uses the scripts), a `/sector-index` landing for the latest quarter, and including consents in L-32's workspace export.
- Migration 110 is a second migration for this package (forward repair under a new number, recorded in PROGRESS).
