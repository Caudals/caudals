Nothing is published by this package. Every public surface is dark (`PUBLIC_RESULTS_ENABLED` off in production), and publishing is a manual founder step after the lawyer's review and the D-16 decision.

## What shipped

- **Written consent (P-23).** On a published report, owners see "Public page" (`components/evals/public-results-consent.tsx`, only with the flag on): they choose a public results page and/or the anonymous sector index, approve the name and a one-sentence scope, pick the index sector (preset from the workspace's L-25 sector), read the versioned consent wording (`2026-10-14`, pending legal review), type their name and role and tick the agreement. The record (`evals.publication_consent`) keeps signer, date, scope, wording version and language; it changes only once, when it is revoked, and is never deleted. Owners withdraw it from the same dialog; a published page goes down in the same transaction. Workspace operators and administrators read; only owners sign (an administrator can record a consent signed outside the app, with a document reference). API `GET/POST/PATCH /api/evals/v1/reports/:id/publication` (404 with the flag off).
- **Founder publication.** `scripts/evals/public-results.ts` (list, preview, publish, unpublish, revoke, record-signed, snapshot-sector) prints SQL for psql; `evals.publish_result_page` (platform administrators) needs a standing consent, a published report, a complete headline and ≥ 10 scored answers, and freezes a projection of dates, scope, sample size, outcome counts, pass rate and 95% interval, review level, scorer version and consent date. No question, answer, finding, source or system text is copied.
- **Public results page** `/{locale}/results/<slug>` (`/es/resultados/<slug>`), Paper reading layout: who chose to publish, test and publication dates, the share that matched the sources with its interval, outcome table with the ink marks, what was tested, sample, review, methodology link and the limits (a sample on the dates shown; agreement with their own sources, not professional correctness; "not a certification, an audit or a conformity assessment"; Caudals endorses no product; revocable). No badge, seal or score artwork. Rendered per request, so a withdrawal is live on the next request; `noindex` until `PUBLIC_RESULTS_INDEXED`.
- **Anonymous sector index (P-53)** `/{locale}/sector-index/<quarter>` (`/es/indice-sectorial/2026-q4`): renders only editions with a recorded legal sign-off and ≥ 10 systems: sector, systems, median share matching the sources, middle half of systems, answers scored, pooled outcome shares; the method and limits are published on the page. Inputs (`evals.sector_index_inputs`) are only consenting customers and our own snapshots with an assigned sector, one row per system with no identifier; the aggregation (`lib/publication/sector-index.ts`) suppresses sectors under 10 and never shows a minimum or maximum. `scripts/evals/sector-index.ts` reads (read-only), previews and, after the sign-off, records; the database recounts the cohort and refuses under 10, an unended quarter, unknown keys or a missing sign-off.
- **Third parties.** Prospect sandboxes can never consent or be published (refused in every function, filtered from the public view); snapshots feed only the anonymous aggregates.
- **Routing.** Translated prefixes for dynamic pages (`PREFIX_PATHS` in `lib/i18n/routing.ts`). No removed route returns. Not in the sitemap, `llms.txt` or agent markdown.
