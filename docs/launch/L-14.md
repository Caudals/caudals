## What shipped

- **Migration 093 `evals_plans_v2`.** `workspace_entitlement.plan` gains `payg` and `partner` (billing modes `credits`); new `max_members` column (owners, editors, viewers and live invitations; operators never count) checked at invitation insert. The 075 system and monthly-run triggers now lock the entitlement row when a cap applies (two inserts at once cannot both pass) and carry `{org_id, limit}` in `DETAIL`; uncapped (managed) workspaces take the old path. `evals.upgrade_plan_on_purchase`, `evals.plan_usage`, `billing.allowance_due`, `billing.workspace_override` with `set_workspace_override` / `list_workspace_overrides` / `live_payments_allowed`. Rollback refuses once a workspace is on payg/partner, has a member cap or an override exists.
- **Free → pay as you go**, atomically: `settlePaymentInTransaction` (webhook, return-page sync, auto-recharge) upgrades right after `billing.record_purchase`, in the same transaction and under the account lock, when plans v2 applies to the workspace. PAYG limits (§6.5): 10 systems, 500 questions per set, no monthly run cap, 2 active runs, schedules, uploads, 10 members; a limit an operator raised is never lowered. Audited `workspace.plan_upgraded` (before/after). Managed and partner workspaces are untouched.
- **Monthly allowance** (`lib/billing/allowance.ts`, evals scheduler every minute while `BILLING_CREDITS_ENABLED=true`): 1,000 credits per free and PAYG workspace and UTC month, `billing.grant` source `allowance` (no price, no cash value), key `allowance:<YYYY-MM>` (once per workspace and month, replays under concurrency), expiring at the next month start; frozen accounts and deleted workspaces get nothing. Only with `PLANS_V2_ENABLED` or the workspace's `plans_v2` override. Work paused for credits resumes after the grant.
- **First-top-up bonus (L-12) verified**: +20 % once per workspace, company domain and tax ID; never stacked with the pack bonus (the larger applies: €500 → +10,000, not +15,000); a refund does not reopen it. Missing tests added.
- **Typed `PLAN_LIMIT_*` errors** (`SYSTEMS`, `RUNS`, `QUESTIONS`, `CONNECTION`, `SCHEDULES`, `MEMBERS`): 403 with `error.limit {kind, limit, connectionType}` and `limit_hit {kind: <code>}` (with the workspace), only where plans v2 applies; otherwise the pre-L-14 generic `PLAN_LIMIT` / `SCOPE_DENIED` answers are unchanged.
- **Upgrade dialog** (`components/evals/limit-dialog.tsx`, mounted in the shell): opened by every typed limit, EN/ES, names the limit, the plan and usage, what credits (PAYG) and a managed plan unlock, "Add credits" (owners where payments are open; editors are told to ask an owner) and "Talk to us" (booking page). Disabled connection cards read "Available with credits" / "Available on a managed plan" with "What unlocks more connections?"; the monitoring notice gets "See what unlocks it". Ops Clients labels PAYG and partner workspaces.
- **Plan summary API** `GET /api/evals/v1/billing/plan?orgId=` (members; 404 while plans v2 is off for the workspace).
- **Rollout overrides** (I-11, L-12 founder note) `GET/PUT /api/evals/v1/billing/admin/overrides` (platform admins, recent sign-in, Idempotency-Key, reason, audited): `enforcement` inherit/shadow/enforce (the engine resolves each workspace's mode in every hook; `BILLING_CREDITS_ENABLED=false` stays the kill switch; a call held in shadow mode settles in shadow mode after a switch; enforcement cannot change while a run is in flight), `live_payments` (live top-ups before Gate B; pages, routes and auto-recharge honour it; `PLANS_V2_ENABLED` opens live top-ups to every owner) and `plans_v2` (plans v2 for one workspace).
