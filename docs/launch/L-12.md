## What shipped

- **Migration 091 `billing_payments`**: `billing_profile`, `stripe_customer`, `top_up_order`, `stripe_event` (event id primary key), `first_bonus_claim`, `auto_recharge`, `spend_cap`, `alert_state`; RLS by `org_id`, read-only for `evals_runtime`, no access for worker roles; writes only through `SECURITY DEFINER` functions (owner functions for owners, admins and services; recording functions for admins and services). Every amount (pack, bonus, VAT, credits, unit price) is derived in the database. `ledger_entry_direction` now allows a negative `refund` that reverses an entry (money refunds). Rollback refuses once payments data exist.
- **Tax** (`lib/billing/tax.ts`): Spanish NIF (CIF, DNI, NIE, K/L/M with control characters) → 21 % IVA; EU VAT ID valid in VIES → reverse charge "Inversión del sujeto pasivo"; EU VAT ID invalid in VIES → 21 % IVA; business outside the EU → "Operación no sujeta"; consumers refused (business declaration and a business tax ID required). VIES is retried (3 attempts), then the profile waits for manual review; Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla also go to review. EU VAT IDs are rechecked before a purchase when the last answer is over 30 days old.
- **Packs and bonus** (`lib/billing/packs.ts`, mirrored in SQL): €20/50/100/250/500 → 2,000/5,150/10,500/27,000/55,000 credits; +20 % on the first top-up once per workspace, company domain (work addresses only) and tax ID, never stacked with the pack bonus (the larger applies; settled at payment).
- **Purchase limits** (§6.15): new accounts (until 30 days after the first payment) can have at most €100 net in flight or kept and start at most 3 top-ups a day; ops lift them per workspace (`billing.lift_purchase_limits`, audited). Card country ≠ tax country and Checkout fiscal details ≠ profile are flagged on the order.
- **Checkout and portal**: hosted Stripe Checkout per order (Stripe customer kept in line with the profile, tax ID added, 21 % Tax Rate when IVA applies, legal mention otherwise, card only, address and tax ID collected, `setup_future_usage=off_session` while no card is saved, idempotency key per order); Terms and Privacy acceptance recorded with context `checkout`; success/cancel return page with live status that also reconciles a completed session itself; Customer Portal limited to payment methods.
- **Webhook** `POST /api/evals/v1/billing/stripe/webhook` (app host, no session): signature verified with the mode's secret, other-mode events refused, every delivery recorded in `billing.stripe_event`, replays answered 200 without effect, out-of-order refunds and disputes reconcile the payment from the PaymentIntent first (or 503 for Stripe to retry). Handles `checkout.session.completed`/`.expired`, `payment_intent.succeeded`/`.payment_failed`, `charge.refunded`, `charge.dispute.created`/`.closed`.
- **Refunds** (`POST /api/evals/v1/billing/admin/refunds`, platform admin with recent sign-in): untouched purchase within 14 days → audited ledger `refund` entries, then the Stripe refund (`refund:<order>`). Above €100 net a second approver is required (L-16). Dashboard refunds are mirrored.
- **Disputes**: freeze on opening, unfreeze when won, `chargeback` entry (never below zero, shortfall flagged) and a lasting freeze when lost.
- **Auto-recharge** (`lib/billing/auto-recharge.ts`, evals scheduler every minute): threshold-crossing state under the account lock, one in flight, Stripe idempotency key `auto-recharge:<workspace>:<crossing>:<attempt>`, monthly cap (count and euros), in-app notice and email on failure (with a 24-hour Checkout link when the bank asks for authentication), one retry after 24 h, then off.
- **Emails through L-07's templates** (`lib/email/templates.tsx`): the `payment_receipt` template gains the top-up's fiscal lines (bonus included, net, IVA or the legal mention, card, validity and refund policy); it goes once per order to the billing email in the billing country's language. Auto-recharge failures use `auto_recharge_failed` and go to the owners who keep billing email on (L-07's recipients), with a 24-hour Checkout link when the bank asks for authentication. Test mode emails only Caudals' own addresses.
- **Analytics**: `top_up_started`, `top_up_paid` (with the workspace's first touch on the first payment), `auto_recharge_paid`, `auto_recharge_failed`.
- **Edge and health**: `proxy.ts` passes the webhook through on the app host; CSP `form-action` allows Stripe Checkout and the Portal; the L-08 webhook health endpoint gains a `stripe` check (key matches mode, signing secret set, no unprocessed event for 15 minutes); unexpected webhook errors go to Sentry.
- **Pages** (owners of workspaces with payments open only; no navigation until L-15): `/workspace/billing/top-up` and `/workspace/billing/top-ups/<order>`.
