## What shipped

- **Migration 091 `billing_payments`**: `billing_profile`, `stripe_customer`, `top_up_order`, `stripe_event` (event id primary key), `first_bonus_claim`, `auto_recharge`, `spend_cap`, `alert_state`. RLS by `org_id`, read-only for `evals_runtime`, nothing for worker roles; writes only through `SECURITY DEFINER` functions (owner functions for owners, admins and services; recording functions for admins and services). Every amount (pack, bonus, VAT, credits, euro unit price) is derived in the database. `ledger_entry_direction` now also allows a negative `refund` that reverses an entry (money refunds). Rollback refuses once any payments row exists.
- **Tax** (`lib/billing/tax.ts`): Spanish NIF (CIF, DNI, NIE, K/L/M, control characters checked) → 21 % IVA; EU VAT ID valid in VIES → reverse charge "Inversión del sujeto pasivo"; EU VAT ID not valid → 21 % IVA; business outside the EU → "Operación no sujeta"; consumers refused (business declaration plus a business tax ID). VIES is retried three times, then the profile waits for manual review; Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla also go to review. EU VAT IDs are rechecked before a purchase when the last answer is older than 30 days.
- **Packs and bonus**: €20/50/100/250/500 → 2,000/5,150/10,500/27,000/55,000 credits; +20 % on the first top-up, once per workspace, company domain (work addresses only) and tax ID, never stacked with the pack bonus (settled at payment).
- **Purchase limits (§6.15)**: until 30 days after the first payment, at most €100 net in flight or kept and 3 top-ups started a day; `billing.lift_purchase_limits` (audited) lifts them. Card country ≠ tax country and Checkout fiscal details ≠ profile are flagged on the order for L-16's fraud queue.
- **Checkout and portal**: hosted Checkout per order (customer kept in line with the profile, tax ID added, 21 % Tax Rate when IVA applies, the legal mention otherwise, card only, address and tax ID collected, `setup_future_usage=off_session` while no card is saved, idempotency key per order); Terms and Privacy acceptance recorded with context `checkout`; success/cancel return page with live status that also reconciles a completed session itself; Customer Portal configuration limited to payment methods.
- **Webhook** `POST /api/evals/v1/billing/stripe/webhook` (app host, no session; `proxy.ts` passes it through): signature verified with the mode's secret, other-mode events refused, each delivery recorded in `billing.stripe_event`, replays answered 200 without effect, out-of-order refunds and disputes reconcile the payment from the PaymentIntent first (or 503 so Stripe retries), permanent mismatches recorded as failed. Handles `checkout.session.completed`/`.expired`, `payment_intent.succeeded`/`.payment_failed`, `charge.refunded`, `charge.dispute.created`/`.closed`. Unexpected errors go to Sentry.
- **Refunds** (`POST /api/evals/v1/billing/admin/refunds`, platform admin with a recent sign-in): untouched purchase within 14 days → audited ledger `refund` entries, then the Stripe refund (`refund:<order>`). Above €100 net a second approver is required (L-16). Refunds made in the Stripe dashboard are mirrored.
- **Disputes**: freeze on opening, unfreeze when won, `chargeback` entry (never below zero, shortfall flagged) and a lasting freeze when lost.
- **Auto-recharge** (`lib/billing/auto-recharge.ts`, evals scheduler every minute): threshold-crossing state under the account lock, one recharge in flight, Stripe idempotency key `auto-recharge:<workspace>:<crossing>:<attempt>`, monthly cap (count and euros), in-app notice, email to owners on failure (24-hour Checkout link when the bank asks for authentication), one retry after 24 h, then off. `PUT /api/evals/v1/billing/auto-recharge` needs a saved card.
- **Emails through L-07's templates**: `payment_receipt` gains the top-up's fiscal lines (bonus included, net, IVA or the legal mention, card, validity and refund policy) and goes once per order to the billing email in the billing country's language; `auto_recharge_failed` goes to owners who keep billing email on. Test mode emails only Caudals' own addresses.
- **Analytics**: `top_up_started`, `top_up_paid` (carrying the workspace's first touch on the first payment), `auto_recharge_paid`, `auto_recharge_failed`.
- **Health and edge**: the L-08 webhook health endpoint gains a `stripe` check (key matches mode, signing secret set, no event unprocessed for 15 minutes); CSP `form-action` allows Stripe Checkout and the Portal.
- **Owner pages** `/workspace/billing/top-up` and `/workspace/billing/top-ups/<order>`: rendered only where payments are open, with no navigation entry (L-15 builds the billing screens).
- **Ops tooling**: `scripts/billing/stripe-webhook-endpoint.ts` (creates the mode's endpoint and stores its secret without printing it) and `scripts/billing/with-app-credentials.sh`. docs/TOOLS.md → Stripe Credit Top-ups; runbook 16.

## Risks and notes

- A negative balance after a lost dispute is not allowed (the L-09 invariant `available ≥ 0` stands): the chargeback removes what is left and records the shortfall for ops.
- New-account limits are cumulative (€100 in flight or kept for 30 days) rather than per order; ops lift them per workspace.
- The receipt language follows the billing country; owner alerts follow each person's email language.
- Concurrent deliveries can both claim the receipt; Resend's idempotency key keeps it to one email (seen once in production: one attempt logged `billing_email_failed`, one email sent).
- Spend caps are stored (`billing.set_spend_cap`) but enforced by L-11/L-15; low-balance, cap and expiry alerts are L-15.
- Invoices are L-13 (`top_up_order.invoice_id` is reserved; the order keeps the fiscal snapshot).
