## 1. What shipped

**1. Settings → Members lists the people in the workspace (R3 S4).**

- `GET /api/evals/v1/workspaces/:id/members` was platform-admin only. It now answers the workspace's owners and operators and platform admins, the same rule as its invitations (`requireWorkspace(..., "manage")`; a platform admin's read is audited `support.manage`), and 404 to editors, viewers and outsiders. Role changes (`PATCH …/members/:userId`) stay with platform admins.
- It reads `evals.list_workspace_people(p_org_id)` (migration 115, SECURITY DEFINER): user id, name, address, role, join date, partner-managed, "is you". The function checks the rule again in SQL (scoped workspace, admin or an owner/operator membership, live workspace) and returns nothing otherwise. The runtime role still cannot read other people's memberships or any auth row directly.
- The tab now opens with **Members** (the people: name or address, a role badge, the join date, a "You" badge, "Partner access" for partner-managed members) and **Create invitation**, then **Invitations** under its own heading (the list, or a one-line empty state). Both tables stack on phones. Platform admins get **Edit role** on each person (the former separate "Workspace roles" table is folded in). The ops client panel shows the same list.
- The help article "Team members and roles" says what the tab lists (EN/ES).

**2. Page headings on phones (R1 #55).** R4's kit rule (under 640 px `.p-head-text` takes the whole row and the actions drop below) already fixed the squeeze; no heading bypasses `PageHeading`. U1 adds the contract that checks it on ten pages in both languages (title as wide as the heading, actions under it and inside it, no canvas overflow) and that a long evaluation title wraps in at most four lines at 390 px and keeps its actions beside it at 1440 px.

**3. `/review/sector-packs` at 390 px (R4 V3).** The pack list and the version's questions use `DataTable stack`: counts labelled ("Expert-approved: 3/62"), the source on its own line. No sideways scroll, in the canvas or in the table.

**4. Fraud queue: top-up velocity (R4 B9).** Migration 115 replaces `billing.fraud_queue` with 094's body plus `peak_24h` on each velocity row: the most top-ups started within any 24 hours of the period, which is what the criterion (3 or more) counts. The row read "0 / 0" for an older burst. The table now shows Most in 24 h · Top-ups (period) · Last 24 h · Failed · Paid (net), sorted by the peak. Risk flags are pills, workspace links are styled (R4 B15).

**5. Expert names for operators (R4 E6).** `evals.expert_name(profile)` (migration 115) returns an expert's account name to platform staff (`evals.is_operator()`) and NULL to anyone else. The review request's assignments (`expert_name`) and the bench (`name`) carry it; the ops screens show the name (the user id on hover), the bench cards, the checklist panel and the order form's checkboxes too. Customer routes never read it.

**6. **7. Admin tables on phones (R3 S8).** The AI model tasks, providers and search engines tables and the own-key tables use `DataTable stack`; in a stacked row a badge row wraps and a model id breaks, so nothing runs past the canvas.

The platform roles help sets `grant-platform-role` as code (R4 PL17).

**9. Quotes in help (R3 H6).** "Plans, limits and billing" and "Schedules and alerts" use “…” in English; Spanish keeps «…» with the period after the closing mark. A new content lint fails on a straight double quote in article prose (code spans excepted) and on “…” in Spanish.
