## What shipped

- **Present.** "Present" in the report heading (`/workspace/reports/:id/present?revision=`) and on the example evaluation (`/workspace/example/present`) for the people `READOUT_MODE_ENABLED` allows. A full-window slide sequence portalled over the shell: cover (system, dates, scope, review status) → verdict (the executive summary, whole-percent score, `pass / scored`, sample line) → causes (the report's "Likely causes" and "Why answers failed" counts out of the scored answers, labelled hypotheses) → five to seven transcripts → "What to fix first" (the report's "Fix these first") → next step (pilot or monthly monitoring, preselected from the plan: `managed` → monitoring, else pilot; editable).
- **Transcripts.** Default: the most severe failure for each cause (likely cause, else what went wrong; causes ranked like the fix list), filled to five with the next failures, then passing answers; at most seven; never an unscored answer by default. Each slide shows the result label and severity, the report's T number (`Result T12`, the same numbering as the PDF/Word evidence appendix), the result id, the likely cause "(hypothesis)", question, answer, the cited source excerpt (or the expected answer), and why. Long text is cut at a word boundary and the slide says "(shortened; full text in the report)".
- **Snapshot only, same numbers.** `lib/evals/reports/readout.ts` builds the deck from the revision snapshot through `buildExecutiveSummary` and `buildFixes`; `readout-document.ts` renders every slide with one HTML template for the screen and the PDF (slide units of 1/1280 of the slide width), so the web and the PDF are the same words and numbers.
- **Keyboard and presenter view.** ← / → / Space (Shift+Space back) / PageUp / PageDown, Home / End, number keys (two quick digits reach 10+), Esc exits to the report, P opens the presenter view in a popup (current and next slide, notes for operators, a timer with pause/reset and the §7.5 segment "Context 0–2 · Failures with their sources 2–10 · What to fix first 10–15 · Next step 15–20 min"), F toggles full screen. Audience and presenter windows stay in step both ways over a `BroadcastChannel` named after the revision; exiting the readout closes the presenter window.
- **Notes and picks (operators).** "Notes and transcripts" side panel: next step, transcript slots (swap, add up to seven, remove, back to default) and a note per slide (default: the §7.5 script; "Reset to the script"). Saved per revision (`PUT /api/evals/v1/reports/:id/readout`, audited as `readout.saved`). Owners and editors present without notes; their panel changes only the next step, for the session.
- **Example.** The example evaluation presents read-only: labelled "Example" with the synthetic-data line on every slide, an operator's notes and picks kept in `localStorage` ("stay on this device"), no PDF export, nothing sent to the API.
- **16:9 PDF.** "Export PDF" queues an `export_job` of kind `readout_pdf` whose `readout` column holds only `{transcript_ids, next_step}` (schema-checked; anything else is refused). The document worker renders it with Chromium at 1280 × 720 px per page (13.33 × 7.5 in), one slide per page, embedded Geist, EN/ES, stored as `readouts/<revision>[.es].<deck hash>.v1.pdf`; the app polls `/exports/:id` and downloads through the report-artifact gateway as `Caudals-<system>-readout-<YYYY-MM-DD>.pdf`. The same deck and language reuse a finished file.
- **Readability.** Body text is 24 slide units (24 px in the PDF, 36 px at 1920 × 1080); labels 18. Below 700 px wide a slide becomes a scrolling card at readable sizes. Reduced motion: slides fade without the 8 px shift.
- **Events.** `readout_presented` (once per person, revision and day) and `readout_exported` (once per new PDF job), server-side, with `revision_hash` (16 hex of the analytics hash) and `slide_count`.

## Migration

`099_evals_readout_decks` (rollback `db/rollbacks/099_evals_readout_decks_down.sql`, refuses while decks, notes or readout jobs exist):
- `evals.is_readout_operator()` (SECURITY DEFINER): platform operator/admin, or the workspace's operator member.
- `evals.readout_deck` (per revision: `transcript_ids` ≤ 7, `next_step`): members read (tenant policy), operators write; `updated_by` must be the actor.
- `evals.readout_note` (per revision and slide key; ≤ 4,000 characters): one policy for every command, operators and platform admins only. Granted to `evals_runtime` only; no worker role has a grant.
- `evals.export_job`: kind `readout_pdf`, column `readout` (checked shape, never shared), index for reuse.
