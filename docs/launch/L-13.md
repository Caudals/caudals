## What shipped

- **Migration 092 `billing_invoices`.** `billing.invoice_series` (`CAU` credits, `CAU-S` services, `CAU-R` rectifying, each with the pattern a live number must match; `confirmed_at` empty until the gestor confirms), `billing.invoice` (one per paid order, `UNIQUE (order_id)`) and `billing.credit_note` (rectifying invoices: one per refunded order, negative amounts, `invoice_id` = the original), plus the security-invoker view `billing.invoice_document`. Each document keeps the buyer's fiscal snapshot (legal name, tax ID and kind, VIES status and consultation, address, billing email), operation date (Madrid), language (ES for Spanish buyers, else EN), net/IVA/gross, treatment and legal mention, lines (purchased credits at 1 cent, bonus credits free of charge), provider and references, Verifactu status, PDF (key, SHA-256, size) and retry state. `top_up_order.invoice_id` now links the invoice (FK). RLS by `org_id`; the runtime reads, never writes; worker roles have no access. Rollback refuses once any document exists.
- **Exactly one invoice per paid order.** A trigger on `billing.top_up_order` queues the invoice when `purchase_entry_id` is first set and the rectifying invoice when the status becomes `refunded` (our refunds and refunds mirrored from the Stripe dashboard), in the same transaction as the payment or refund, whichever function moves the order (L-14's upgrade path is unaffected). Replays and concurrent deliveries give one document. Partial refunds and chargebacks are left to the accountant (flagged in the export). The migration backfilled the two refunded L-12 test orders.
- **Tax lines** per treatment, checked in the database and equal to `lib/billing/tax.ts`: Spain 21 % IVA (both tax IDs; the issuer's from `LEGAL_ENTITY_*`), EU with valid VIES "Inversión del sujeto pasivo", outside the EU "Operación no sujeta".
- **Adapter interface** `lib/billing/invoicing/index.ts` (`syncCustomer`, `issueInvoice`, `issueRectifying` with the original's number and reference, `fetchPdf`; idempotent per document id) and **the `manual` adapter** (`manual.ts`): every request becomes "pending issue" for the accountant; it never makes up a number or a PDF. Live numbers must match their series; Stripe test-mode documents accept only `TEST-` numbers, and only they can be voided. A fake vendor (`tests/billing/fake-invoicing-provider.ts`) proves the interface (customer sync, series numbers, PDFs, retries).
- **Invoicing job and retry queue** (`jobs.ts`, evals scheduler every minute): due documents (invoices before rectifying invoices) are claimed with a 10-minute lease, handed to the adapter and recorded; failures stay queued with a 1, 2, 4 … 60-minute backoff. **Alert** `invoicing_stalled` in `scripts/evals/health-alerts.sh` (runbook 22): a document queued an hour after a provider failure, or a day without any attempt; included in the drill.
- **Ops API** (platform admin, sign-in within 15 minutes, `Idempotency-Key`, reason, audited `billing.invoice_issued` / `billing.invoice_voided`): `GET /api/evals/v1/billing/admin/invoices?status=&mode=`; `POST /api/evals/v1/billing/admin/invoices/:id` `{action: "issue", orgId, kind, number, issueDate, pdfBase64, verifactuStatus?, reason}` (number checked against the series, date not before the operation or the original's date and not in the future, a rectifying invoice only after its original, PDF ≤ 5 MB sealed in private storage) and `{action: "void", reason}` (test mode only). Issuing emails the billing contact once with the PDF attached (`invoice_available`, L-07 template, EN/ES; test mode only to Caudals' own addresses). The L-16 console builds on these.
- **Owner API** (owner only, server-side role checks; platform admins read with an audited support check; 404 while invoicing is off): `GET /api/evals/v1/billing/invoices?orgId=` and `GET /api/evals/v1/billing/invoices/:id/pdf?orgId=` (verified PDF from private storage, or the provider's, kept on first download). The Invoices tab is L-15.
- **Monthly accountant export** `scripts/billing/monthly-export.ts` (`billing.accountant_export(month, livemode)`, rendering in `lib/billing/invoicing/export.ts`): sales by treatment (net, IVA, gross, credits), refunds, chargebacks, the purchased-credit roll-forward at the price paid (opening deferred balance, purchased, consumed, returned, refunded, expired, charged back, adjusted, closing), free-credit movements, orders, and every document of the month or still to issue with full fiscal data, lines in its language and the expected series; checks; reconciliation to Stripe balance transactions (by charge, refund or PaymentIntent; fees, net and payouts). Formula-safe CSVs. Usage in docs/TOOLS.md → Invoicing.
- **Placeholder CI check extended** (`lib/legal/documents.test.ts`, part of the Docker release gate): the invoicing module, the export script, the email copy and templates carry none of the §3.2 values, and the rendered export never shows a placeholder issuer.
- Docs: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (Billing: Invoicing and IVA), `docs/TOOLS.md` (Invoicing and the Monthly Accountant Export), `docs/evals/runbooks.md` (22. Invoicing backlog, drills).

## Risks and notes

- Until a vendor exists, live invoices depend on the monthly manual cycle; the export lists every document still to issue, and `invoicing_stalled` alerts only on provider failures or invoicing off for a day.
- Voiding exists only for Stripe test mode; a live mistake is corrected with a rectifying invoice (needs a small ops path or the vendor).
- Free-credit movements in the export label money-refunded bonus credits as "other".
- The scheduler has no `LEGAL_ENTITY_*`: a vendor adapter that needs the issuer will need them passed to the workers.
