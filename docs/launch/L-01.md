## What shipped

- **Legal texts rewritten in EN and ES** (`content/legal/{en,es}.json`) for what Caudals does now: the evaluation SaaS, the free public test (deleted after 7 days; commercial-model fallback disclosed), the free plan, credits (1 credit = €0.01 excl. VAT; purchased credits 24 months, promotional 30 days, allowance to month end, 14-day refund of an untouched purchase; D-10), acceptable use (own or authorized systems only, ordinary questions, no adversarial tests without written authorization), business customers only (D-05), liability, Spanish law. Every marketplace concept (buyers, suppliers, payouts, dataset workflow) is gone. No certification or compliance claims.
- **New pages** `/legal/dpa` (summary + downloadable Word template, EN and ES), `/legal/subprocessors`, `/legal/security`, with Spanish slugs `/es/legal/encargo-de-tratamiento`, `/es/legal/subencargados`, `/es/legal/seguridad` (`TRANSLATED_PATHS`). All seven legal pages share a new Paper reading layout (`components/legal/legal-page.tsx`): document list, "Last updated" and version, numbered anchored sections, contents on long documents, responsive tables.
- **Template download**: `/legal/caudals-dpa-en.docx`, `/legal/caudals-dpa-es.docx` (`app/legal/[file]/route.ts`, generated per request from the same content with `docx`).
- **Footer, sitemap, agent markdown and `llms.txt`** list all seven legal pages from one list (`lib/legal/versions.ts`). Sitemap `lastmod` is each document's version date.
- **Company data (I-1)**: `LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME`, `LEGAL_ENTITY_NIF`, `LEGAL_ENTITY_ADDRESS`, `LEGAL_ENTITY_REGISTRY` read at request time (`lib/legal/entity.ts`); allowlisted in `scripts/set-app-credentials.sh`. Unset: the notice names the trade name "Caudals" and says the company details will be published on registration, with the contact address. §3.2 placeholder values are ignored at runtime. The legal notice is the only legal page rendered per request.
- **Version constants (I-2)**: `lib/legal/versions.ts`, every document at `2026-10-11` (cookies keeps `2026-03-01`, text unchanged) and marked `pending_legal_review` internally.
- **Terms acceptance at sign-up**: required checkbox on `/workspace/signup` linking to Terms and Privacy in the person's language; `/api/evals/v1/signup` refuses with `TERMS_REQUIRED` without it and records `terms` and `privacy` with their versions, locale and request id, in the same transaction that creates the account. Behind `SIGNUP_TERMS_REQUIRED`.
- **Migration 081 `evals_terms_acceptance`**: append-only `evals.terms_acceptance` (UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE refused by trigger), RLS read policy (self, workspace members, platform admins), `evals.record_terms_acceptance()` SECURITY DEFINER (records for the tenant actor, checks membership, idempotent per request id, format-checked document/context so L-12 `checkout` and L-30 `dpa` need no schema change). Rollback refuses while rows exist.
- **CI placeholder check**: `lib/legal/documents.test.ts` (runs in CI's `npm test -- --run`) fails on bracketed fill-ins or any §3.2 value in `content/legal/*`, and on certification/compliance claims or marketplace terms.
