## What shipped

- **Panel.** "Ask this report" (secondary action in the report heading) opens a side panel on `/workspace/reports/:id` for the revision being read: the typical cost and limits before the first question, suggestions, the thread, and the field. Every answer sentence is followed by T (result) and F (finding) citations that close the panel and open the result (`?result=`) or the finding (Findings tab, focused). Fixed refusal with "How we evaluate" and "Book a readout"; "I can't answer that from this report."; failures say nothing was charged. Viewers read; owners and operators delete. Not on share links or the example.
- **Rules** (`lib/evals/reports/questions.ts`, `question-refs.ts`): snapshot-only quoted packet (≤ 40 KB, angle brackets escaped), T/F references as in the PDF and Findings tab, the validator (a whole answer is discarded when a sentence is uncited or cites a reference not in the snapshot), the output filter ("compliant"/"certified" and Spanish forms → fixed refusal), the scope rule (compliance, certification and legal conclusions refused before any model call), the language of the question.
- **Engine path** (`lib/evals/repositories/report-questions.ts`, `lib/evals/reports/question-worker.ts`, `lib/evals/queue/worker.ts`): one `report_writer` step per question on the report's frozen model route (free: local only), JSON output, 700 output tokens, 180 s (DGX) / 120 s (commercial), no retries, a five-minute queue deadline; one hold `attempt:<id>` captured (completed call) or released (failure, timeout, crash), settled in the same transaction as the question.
- **Migration 106** `evals_report_questions`.
- **API** `GET/POST /api/evals/v1/reports/:id/questions`, `GET/DELETE …/questions/:questionId`.
- **Event** `report_question_asked {outcome, credits}`.

## Decisions

- A completed call is captured even when its answer is discarded or refused by the model (it is a completed engine call, §6.3, and the shadow-week check `completed_without_capture` stays 0); failures, timeouts and crashes are released at once; a fixed refusal makes no call.
- Viewers can read questions and answers but not ask (asking spends credits, like starting a run).
- Questions are per revision; the daily cap is per report (all revisions).

## Release evaluation (local DGX model, no paid calls)

`scripts/evals/report-questions-release.ts` with the fixed set in `scripts/evals/report-questions-release-set.ts` on the example evaluation (L-33, October run, EN and ES), model `bluehawana/deepseek-v4-flash:iq2_m` (Caudals Local, the production report-writer route), the product's prompt, the engine's adapter and the product's validator, one call at a time, 2026-10-14.

| Run | Prompt | In scope (50) | Valid citations | Out of scope (15) | Injection probes (2) | Latency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `caudals-report-questions-v1` | 40 answered, 10 "can't answer" (5 sentences citing all 48 results, over the 20-reference cap; 5 citing "improvements", "methodology" or "report_data") | 242 / 250 = **96.8 %** | **15 / 15 refused** (6 by rule, 9 by the model) | not steered | p50 8 s, max 54 s |
| 2 (shipped) | `caudals-report-questions-v2` | **50 answered**, 0 "can't answer" | 630 / 630 = **100 %** | **15 / 15 refused** (6 by rule, 9 by the model) | not steered (both answered from the report) | p50 7.5 s, p90 17.7 s, max 55 s |

Tuning between the runs (recorded honestly): run 1 met both targets, but all ten "can't answer" replies came from references the validator rightly refused: five sentences about whole-report numbers cited all 48 results (over the 20-reference cap) and five cited "improvements", "methodology" or "report_data", which have no id of their own. Prompt v2 says T and F are the only references and how to cite a whole-report number (the results it counts), an improvement (its findings) and the method (the results or findings it concerns); a sentence may cite up to 200 references (the panel folds them after six). The validator and the scope rule are unchanged. Run 2 tokens: median 8.8 k input (the Ollama prefix cache makes later questions on the same report fast) and 103 output. No answer shown contained "compliant" or "certified". Reading the answers: they are accurate to the example; one (Is this report reviewed?) left out that the example's review is simulated.
