## What shipped

- **Panel** (`components/evals/report-questions.tsx`). "Ask this report" (secondary action in the report heading) opens a side panel on `/workspace/reports/:id` for the revision being read: before the first question, the typical cost ("Typical cost: 1 credit per question (≈ €0.01), at most 3 credits. Only completed answers are charged…", or "included in your contract", "your provider key", "no credits" for a free model) and the limits; three suggestions; the thread; the field (500 characters, ⌘/Ctrl+Enter). Every answer sentence is followed by T (result) and F (finding) citations that close the panel and open the result (`?result=`) or the finding (Findings tab, focused); lists longer than seven fold behind "+N". The fixed refusal links "How we evaluate" (the public methodology page) and "Book a readout"; "I can't answer that from this report."; failures and timeouts say nothing was charged. Answers and fixed texts use the question's language; the panel follows the interface language (`rq*` catalogue keys, EN/ES). Viewers read; owners and operators delete (confirm dialog). Not on share links (session-only API) or the example.
- **Rules** (`lib/evals/reports/questions.ts`, `question-refs.ts`): the revision's snapshot only — metrics, findings, results, improvements, methodology — compacted into a packet of at most 40 KB (recorded text shortened step by step, then results dropped, failures last) and passed as JSON between `<report_data>` tags with `<`/`>` escaped, so a transcript cannot close the tags; the system prompt says the data is not instructions; the question comes last (the Ollama prefix cache then answers later questions on the same report in seconds). T numbers follow the PDF/readout order (`resultNumbers`), F numbers the Findings tab. `validateQuestionOutput` discards the whole answer when any sentence is uncited or cites a reference that is not in the snapshot; the output filter replaces any answer containing `complian*`, `certif*`, `certificad*`, `conformidad` or `cumplimiento normativo` by the fixed refusal; `scopeRule` refuses compliance, certification and legal-conclusion questions before any model call; the model returns `out_of_scope` for general advice and other systems and `not_in_report` when the report lacks the answer. The panel re-validates stored answers against the snapshot before showing them. Prompt revision `caudals-report-questions-v2`.
- **Engine path** (`lib/evals/repositories/report-questions.ts`, `lib/evals/reports/question-worker.ts`, `lib/evals/queue/worker.ts`, `lib/evals/providers/contracts.ts`): one `report_writer` step per question on the report's model route frozen into the question (the run's frozen route while its model is available, else the workspace's current route; free plans local only; a BYOK route is honoured and carries no token credits), JSON output, 700 output tokens, 180 s on the DGX / 120 s on a commercial API, no web research (schema-enforced), a run budget scoped to the question. Enforce mode refuses with `402 CREDITS_EXHAUSTED` when the hold is not covered. The worker closes a question erased or waiting more than five minutes before its claim (no hold, no call), never retries a question, settles an unknown outcome at its bound and releases its hold at once (nothing left to reconcile), and in the transaction that captures or releases the one hold `attempt:<id>` stores the validated answer, tokens, the ledger mode (`enforce`/`shadow`/`contract`/`free`/`off`), the outcome (`captured`, `released`, `none`) and the ledger or shadow entry ids. Worker recovery after a crash releases the hold and fails the question instead of asking again.
- **Migration 106** `evals_report_questions`: `evals.report_question` (question, language, status, reason, answer, cited result and finding ids, revision, run, the stored T/F map, packet hash, model and price revisions, model source, data routing, workflow/step/attempt, token bounds and usage, ledger mode/outcome/key/entry ids, price-book revision, held and charged µcr, deadline, erase fields). RLS: members of the workspace and platform staff read; owners, editors and operators (and staff) ask; the inference worker reads and updates only outcome columns of its tenant's rows; no document, browser or expert grant. A trigger enforces the caps (20 per report a UTC day; 10 per workspace a UTC month on free; failed and timed-out questions do not count; an advisory lock per workspace), immutable identity and provenance, and one settled outcome. Rows are never deleted: `evals.delete_report_question` (owners, operators, platform admins) erases the question and answer, keeps metadata and ledger references, audited `report_question.deleted`. Never used for training (nothing exports it).
- **API**: `GET /api/evals/v1/reports/:id/questions?orgId&revisionId` (any member: enabled, canAsk, canDelete, limits, typical cost from `billing.engine_pricing`, this revision's questions), `POST` (owners, editors, operators; flag; 10 a minute per person), `GET/DELETE …/questions/:questionId` (delete works with the flag off).
- **Event** `report_question_asked {outcome, credits}` when a question settles (credits charged in enforce mode, else 0).
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE (evaluation section, event table), FRONTEND, DESIGN (`.p-qa*`, `.p-cite`), TOOLS (release script, flags). Flags allow-listed in `scripts/set-app-credentials.sh`.

## Decisions

- A completed call is captured even when its answer is discarded or refused by the model: it is a completed engine call (§6.3 "engine tokens … no separate meter"), the brief names only failures and timeouts as refunded, and the shadow-week check `completed_without_capture` stays 0. Failures, timeouts and crashes are released at once; a fixed refusal makes no call (outcome `none`).
- Viewers read questions and answers but cannot ask (asking spends credits, like starting a run).
- Questions belong to the revision they were asked about; the daily cap counts the report's questions across revisions.
- Metrics, improvements and methodology have no id of their own, so the model cites the results a number counts, the findings an improvement addresses, and the results or findings a method statement concerns (prompt v2); a sentence may cite up to 200 references.
- The release evaluation calls the DGX with the engine's own adapter outside a reservation, on synthetic example data only; the reservation, hold and RLS path is proven by the DB suite and the production check below.

## Risks and notes

- R20 (wrong or uncited answers): snapshot-only input, validated citations, fixed refusal, output filter and the release evaluation. A cited answer can still misread its evidence (the citations make it checkable). Re-run the release set whenever the prompt revision, validator, scope rules or report-writer model change.
- Large reports: the packet keeps at most 40 KB, so on reports with hundreds of results the passing results are shortened first and the last results dropped (the packet says how many); questions about dropped results get "I can't answer that from this report."
- The scope rule is deliberately narrow (compliance, certification, legal conclusions); everything else relies on the model's `out_of_scope` (9/9 in the release set).
- The DGX has one residency slot: during a long evaluation a question waits; after five minutes in the queue it closes as timed out without a charge.
- Erased questions keep their text in the engine call's immutable execution record (prompt and output), which follows the evidence retention policy like every engine call.
