## Implemented behaviour

- **Price-book snapshot (migration 089).** A trigger freezes `price_book_revision_id` (the revision in effect) into every `evals.run` and `evals.generation_job` at insert, next to `model_routes`; changing it raises `immutable job price book`. Older rows are priced at the revision in effect when they were created.
- **Engine hooks** (`lib/billing/engine.ts`, inside the existing transactions, after the euro budget locks: provider account → workspace and run budgets → `billing.credit_account` → lots):
  - `InvocationWorker.claim()`: after `reserve()`, a hold `attempt:<id>` of model rate × the call's token bound (generator, context analyzer, judge, report writer; never probes or target calls).
  - Success: after `settle()`, a capture of rate × reported usage (or the prompt bound and answer bytes, capped at the output bound, when no usage is reported), capped at the hold, rest released. Incomplete answers are released. Completed web searches add `search:<attempt>`.
  - Release: `finishFailure` (known failures), cancel before dispatch, `recover()` for undispatched calls. Unknown outcomes keep the hold; `billing.expire_due` releases it after 24 h.
  - Platform meters (`unit:<case_unit_id>`): website/API questions on a `succeeded` observation (target worker), API questions from private runners, graded imported answers in `scoreRun`, finalized sources (`source:<revision>`: website pages, PDF pages, or 3,000 characters a page). Never for incomplete, timed-out, failed or unknown units.
  - Pricing via `billing.engine_pricing` / `billing.action_pricing` (SECURITY DEFINER, tenant-checked): mode, balance snapshot, owning run (run workflow, judge job or narrative job) and its frozen revision.
- **Enforce mode** (`BILLING_ENFORCE=enforce`): `createSelfServiceRun` (start, schedule dispatch, restarts, later CI) refuses with `402 CREDITS_EXHAUSTED` below the estimator's p90; resume needs the p90 of the questions left. `CreditsExhausted` at a claim, a target claim that cannot pay the next question, or a partly paid meter pauses the workflow with `credits_exhausted` ("Paused — add credits to continue", EN/ES); `controlRun(resume)` and `resumeCreditPausedWork` re-queue. Contract workspaces never hold credits. `cap_reached` is reserved (label and pause path) for L-12/L-14 caps.
- **Shadow mode** (`BILLING_ENFORCE=shadow`): the same amounts go to `billing.shadow_entry` (append-only, RLS, no FK to the account), contract workspaces included and flagged. Each write runs in its own savepoint with a 500 ms lock timeout, never locks the credit account, never raises for a short balance (`would_block`) and swallows errors (`billing_shadow_error`). Admissions are recorded by the scheduler for runs started in the last 5 minutes (off the request path).
- **Scheduler** (hourly while credits are on): shadow holds unsettled after 24 h are released; failed runs (no usable result) with charges are refunded once (`run-refund:<run_id>`, real and shadow). Daily at 04:00 UTC (and at start) `billing.record_shadow_report` for the previous UTC day (`billing_shadow_report`, or `billing_shadow_mismatch` as an error). The health check alerts `billing_shadow_drift` (runbook 17) when the latest report has mismatches. `scripts/billing/shadow-report.ts` prints the diff on demand.
- **Monthly workspace budget (G8).** `execution_budget.period_start`/`period_base`: the workspace ceiling applies to `settled − period_base`; the first `reserve()`/`settle()` of a UTC month moves the base. Backfilled to October 2026. Run budgets and provider accounts keep lifetime totals; the admin ceiling update uses the window.

## Known limitations and next work

- Shadow admissions are recorded only for runs started while the scheduler sees shadow mode (5-minute window) and not for resumes; enforce admission covers both.
- A grading or report pass paused for credits (enforce) has no run-level banner yet; resuming the run (or `resumeCreditPausedWork` after a top-up) re-queues it. L-15 adds the "Paused — add credits" state with top-up and resume.
- Per-run and monthly caps (`cap_reached`) arrive with L-12/L-14; the pause path and labels are ready.
- Source page counts: website sections, PDF page markers, otherwise 3,000 characters a page.
