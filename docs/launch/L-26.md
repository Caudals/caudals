## What shipped

- **Request (P-10).** "Have an expert review this" appears in the report's next steps, replacing L-17's contact link while the flag is on. It also appears in the evaluation's results summary.
  - It is shown to owners and workspace operators, on a published, current report.
  - A short modal asks for scope (failed and partly correct answers, the whole report, findings and fixes, or a pilot), an optional note, a deadline and the sector.
  - `POST /api/evals/v1/reports/:id/expert-review` calls `evals.request_expert_review`. That opens **one request per report** (a second click returns it) and records the reply SLA (`reply_due_at`: the next working day, Europe/Madrid, weekends skipped).
  - The same call writes an immutable **Warmbly CRM draft** (`evals.review_crm_draft`, operators only; the table checks `sent: false` and `destination: warmbly`).
  - The route then sends **one email to the founders** at the internal `COLLABORATION_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL`, with a Resend idempotency key and no answers or contact addresses.
  - The customer then sees "Requested — we'll reply within one working day", later the order's due date and then the delivery. They never see quotes or orders.
  - Client event: `expert_review_requested {surface: report|results}`.
- **Order (P-32).** `/ops/experts` has new tabs, Review requests (list with reply/due SLA badges) and Bench. `/ops/experts/reviews/:id` shows:
  - the request, the CRM draft as a copy block, "Mark quote sent" and "Decline";
  - the order form: scope shown on the report, sector, due date, **hand-written quote** in EUR with a note (never credits), ready experts, the results in scope (failed and partial preselected) and a conflict-of-interest confirmation;
  - a **required preview of the redacted excerpts** before "Create order and assignments".

  One Stage E `authoring` assignment is created per expert, with a guideline revision (EN/ES, confidentiality, DPA and IP terms) and the same redacted excerpts. `lib/evals/experts/redaction.ts` builds each excerpt from the question, the answer, the automatic grade with its reasoning, the expected answer and up to two reference excerpts. It removes:
  - emails, links and website names;
  - phone, card and account numbers, IBANs and DNI/NIE/CIF numbers;
  - the workspace, system and report names;
  - every result, source and customer id.

  `evals.link_review_order_assignment` refuses an expert whose checklist is incomplete, and the whole order rolls back. `evals.review_order_assignment` (operators only, immutable) is the only link from an assignment to its order.
- **Reviewed revision.** "Publish the reviewed revision" creates a new immutable revision of the current report and publishes it.
  - The revision carries `expert_review {order_id, reviewer_count, reviewed_on, scope, note?}`. The summary shows **"Reviewed by N experts on <date>, scope …"** on the web, PDF and Word, and shared copies carry it with the scope section.
  - The workspace gets a `report_published` notice with `expertReviewers`, and opted-in members get the L-07 email "Experts reviewed your Caudals report" (EN/ES).
  - N counts distinct experts who submitted their own work. A `BEFORE INSERT` trigger on `report_revision` refuses any label with fewer recorded reviewers (**I-7**).
  - Grades and `review_status` are unchanged. Ops settle disagreements in the review queue first.
  - Time to review is recorded (`review_order.delivered` audit with `hours_to_review`, and on the order page).
- **Bench (P-33).** `evals.expert_onboarding` records:
  - credentials;
  - a contract with IP assignment and confidentiality;
  - the DPA;
  - a paid trial against gold items, with agreement and threshold (default 80 %);
  - when the trial was paid (tracked by hand);
  - sectors (the roster), availability and hours.

  `evals.expert_ready` gates order work. The Bench tab shows what each expert is missing, with a side panel to edit the checklist. Assignment payments stay manual (WP-14 payment records). There are no payouts and no marketplace.
- **Expert screens (§5.4).**
  - Every literal is localized (EN/ES).
  - Open work is sorted by due date, with overdue and due-within-a-day badges.
  - The workbench shows its due badge and a visible save state (unsaved, saving, saved at…, not saved) in an `aria-live` span.
  - Shortcuts: Ctrl/⌘+S saves the draft, Ctrl/⌘+Enter submits or records the decision, and Alt+G jumps to the guideline. A "Read the guideline" link and a methodology link sit beside them.
- **Stage E release check.** It now reports `reviewOrders: ready | not_installed` (migration 101 and its three triggers) and fails when `EXPERT_REVIEW_ORDERS_ENABLED=true` without them.
- **Docs.** ARCHITECTURE (feature contract, L-17 link note, event row), FRONTEND, DESIGN (classes and change log), TOOLS (release check in production), evals AGENTS.

## Migration 101 `evals_review_orders`

Additive.

- **Tables** (all with forced RLS): `review_request`, readable by members and operators; and four operator-only tables: `review_crm_draft`, `review_order`, `review_order_assignment` and `expert_onboarding`.
- **Writes** go only through `SECURITY DEFINER` functions, which also write the audit trail: `request_expert_review`, `mark_review_request_notified`, `update_review_request`, `create_review_order`, `link_review_order_assignment`, `review_order_progress`, `complete_review_order`, `cancel_review_order` and `save_expert_onboarding`.
- **Helpers:** `next_working_day`, `expert_ready` and `review_order_recorded_reviewers` (not granted).
- **Triggers:** the I-7 guard, and immutable triggers on the CRM draft and the order links.
- **Rollback** refuses while any of the five tables holds rows (tested).

## Not done (and why)

- **Creating expert profiles in the UI.** Profiles are still created through the restricted operator API (`POST /api/evals/v1/experts`, WP-14). The bench edits the checklist of existing profiles.
- **Expert verdicts changing grades automatically.** Ops read the submissions and settle grades in the review queue before publishing. The label states who reviewed what, not that grades changed.
- **Direct Warmbly push.** The draft is copied by hand, as in L-21 (founder decision in §5).
- **Public holidays** are not modelled in the one-working-day reply SLA.
- **Not checked in production:** the signed-in report and results pages with the button. No production session is available to agents; the harness contracts and the production lib path cover it.

## Founder actions

- Recruit, vet and contract real experts (P-33). For each expert:
  - create the profile through the operator API;
  - record in Ops → Expert work → Bench: credentials, a signed contract with IP assignment and confidentiality, the DPA, a paid trial against gold items above the agreement threshold, sectors and availability.

  Until one expert is ready, requests still arrive (with the founders' email) but no order can be created.
- Run the quote process. Reply to each request within one working day with a hand-written quote. Record it with "Mark quote sent". Create the order once it is accepted, and invoice by hand (never credits, §6.17). Confirm with the gestor how expert review is invoiced (the `CAU-S-` services series, L-13). The flag is on, so owners can request today: watch the internal mailbox and `/ops/experts`.

## Risks

- **Access leaks.** Experts reach only the WP-14 projection of their own assignment. The order, request, draft and checklist tables are invisible to them, by tests and in production. Redaction is pattern-based, so the order form requires a preview: person names in free text are the operator's to catch.
- **Expert capacity.** No real expert is onboarded yet, and the SLA is manual: reply within one working day, and orders have due dates with overdue badges.
- **Overclaiming.** The label names the count, date and scope. `review_status` never changes, and the trigger enforces recorded reviewers.
