## What shipped

- **Sector packs (P-30, P-31).** Platform data outside every workspace: pack → version (`draft` → `in_review` → `published`) → items. Each item is one sector-generic question with its expected answer, key facts (spans of the excerpt), severity and difficulty, the cited source (https URL, title, publisher, locator, verbatim excerpt, retrieval date = freshness, optional as-of date), rights (`public_official_text`, `public_sector_reuse`, `licensed` with a licence reference, or `caudals_owned`, always with the basis), author, last editor and the reviewing expert (Stage E `evals.expert_profile`; L-26 had not landed, so the existing roster is the reviewer reference).
- **Guards in the database.** `scope = 'sector_generic'` on every item; customer-owned rights are not a valid kind; the writer functions refuse inside a workspace context and any payload naming a workspace or case; one question fingerprint per version (deduplication); a published version and its items are immutable even for the table owner; publication needs every item `approved` by an eligible expert with reviewer, profile and time recorded; reviewers are never the author or last editor; an edit resets the review. RLS: staff and verified experts see drafts, everyone else only published versions; runtime has SELECT only and writes go through audited SECURITY DEFINER functions. The app also requires 60 approved questions to publish.
- **Authoring.** Ops library **Sector packs** tab (`/ops/library/sector-packs`, staff): pack list (version, status, approved/total, oldest source with a "Check source" badge past 180 days), import of a `caudals.sector-pack/v1` file, version page with the pending-review banner, review progress, search/topic/review filters, question panel (excerpt, source link, rights and basis, author, reviewer, anonymous results), add/edit/remove, submit, back to draft, publish (with confirmation) and new version (copies the published one with its reviews). `/review/sector-packs` for verified experts (draft and edit; eligible experts approve, request changes or reject with a note). Anonymous per-item results across workspaces (`evals.sector_pack_item_stats`: counts only, outcome counts withheld below three workspaces).
- **"Add N sector questions".** In Prepare (above the test-set review) and on a test set: asks the workspace's sector once (owners, editors, operators), then offers the newest **published** pack for that sector. Adding writes the next test-set version (a frozen set is frozen again as a new version): the pack becomes a frozen source of the project (one excerpt per item with its citation line, private file for 12 months) and each missing question a case modelled on the set's first question, with `sector_generic` tag and provenance (`human_authored`, `expert_reviewed` with the reviewer, rights mapped, extension `caudals.evals/sector-pack`) and a link row to the pack item. Idempotent; questions already in the set are skipped; the plan's question cap applies.
- **Monthly new questions (P-26).** Opt-in per workspace (owners and operators; managed, PAYG and partner plans) on the test set page. Every five minutes the scheduler (flag on) takes the opted-in workspaces and, once per source and calendar month, queues a new reading of each live website source of an evaluation with an approved test set whose last reading predates the month (existing browser reader, egress proxy and document worker; same start page, page limit and L-34 page selection; at most 10 sources a month). The new revision is diffed page by page by content hash; excerpts of new and changed pages go to one generator call on the evaluation's route through the reservation path (workspace and refresh budgets, shadow credit holds); validated, source-grounded questions become proposals (≤ 12 per source, ≤ 25 per workspace and month). Owners, editors and operators approve proposals into a new test-set version (cases cite the new revision; provenance `synthetic` with generator and prompt revision, extension `caudals.evals/monthly-proposal` with refresh, page and approver) or reject them; decisions are audited and immutable.
- **Seed content (drafts, I-7).** `content/sector-packs/es-fiscal-contable-laboral.json` (ICP-1: tax, accounting and payroll, Spain) and `content/sector-packs/es-seguros.json` (insurance, Spain): **70 + 70 questions**, Spanish with an English gloss, each from a BOE consolidated text fetched on 2026-10-13 with its verbatim excerpt (checked programmatically against the saved page text), locator, retrieval and as-of dates, and rights (139 `public_official_text` under art. 13 TRLPI; 1 `public_sector_reuse` for a BOE editorial note under the BOE reuse notice). Every item says "Seed drafted by a Caudals agent … Pending expert review." No licensed, paywalled or commercial content. Sources: Ley 37/1992 IVA, Ley 35/2006 IRPF and RD 439/2007, Ley 27/2014 IS, Ley 58/2003 LGT, RD 1619/2012 facturación, Código de Comercio, RDL 1/2010 LSC, RDL 2/2015 ET, RDL 8/2015 LGSS; Ley 50/1980 LCS, RDL 8/2004, RDL 3/2020, Ley 44/2002 (as amended by Ley 10/2025), Ley 22/2007, RDL 7/2004, Ley 20/2015.
- **Ops CLI** `services/evals-worker/sector-packs.ts` (in the worker image; pack files on stdin; counts and id prefixes only): `validate`, `import`, `refresh-start`, `refresh-retry`, `refresh-advance`, `proposals`, `reject-proposals` (docs/TOOLS.md → Sector Packs and Monthly New Questions).

## Risks and notes

- Reviewer notes from drafting (ICP-1): the 0,26 €/km mileage figure comes from a BOE editorial note (Orden HFP/792/2023), not the regulation's own text; the 2026 corporate tax rates for small and micro companies come from transitional provision 44ª; mandatory B2B e-invoicing (art. 8 bis) applies under Ley 56/2007's development rules, phase-in not in the excerpt; unfair-dismissal pay is hedged "con carácter general"; LGT and LSC texts were last updated in 2024/2025; only the common state regime (no foral or regional rules). Insurance: complaint items follow Ley 44/2002 as rewritten by Ley 10/2025 (12-month adaptation to about 2026-12-28; confirm for insurers); distance-contract items cite Ley 22/2007 (unchanged since 2018; EU 2023/2673 transposition pending); one excerpt (`distancia-seguros-sin-desistimiento`) is the exclusion list without its lead-in; motor limits are as the current text states.
- Re-reads re-meter pages read (`web_page_read`) and the drafting call holds and captures credits like any generation call (shadow today).
- Freezing a new version of an old test set fails when one of its original source files has passed the 90-day retention; the questions then stay in the draft and the response says so (existing retention behaviour).
- The browser reader's file path for a later reading carries the website job id; the first reading keeps its original path.
- Recovery: `scripts/launch/set-app-flag.sh SECTOR_PACKS_ENABLED false` (then redeploy the workers at the running commit) hides every surface and stops the monthly job; the schema is additive.
