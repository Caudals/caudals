## What shipped

- **Schedules on credits** (`lib/evals/monitoring/schedules.ts`, `schedule-credits.ts`, `projection.ts`): any workspace whose entitlement has `can_schedule` can schedule; pay as you go gets it with plans v2 (L-14), managed by contract. Workspaces that pay with credits schedule **weekly or monthly** (`422` for daily; contracts keep daily and their euro run spend). Owners may give a schedule a **credit limit per month** (`billing.spend_cap` scope `schedule`, written only through `billing.set_spend_cap`: owners, audited `billing.spend_cap_saved`; changing it keeps the schedule's version). `POST /schedules {creditCapCredits}`, `PATCH /schedules/:id {creditCapCredits | null}`; `GET /schedules` returns `credit_cap_ucr`.
- **Each due slot is checked before its run exists** (in a savepoint, inside the dispatch): the schedule's month spend (captures and meters of its runs minus refunds plus open holds) plus the run's p90 against its limit, then the balance or an auto-recharge that covers it against the p90, then the workspace's monthly and fixed per-run caps. What it saw is stored on `schedule_dispatch.admission` (mode, p90, available, limit, month spend, verdict). Where credits are enforced (`BILLING_ENFORCE` or the workspace override) a short slot is **skipped** with `credits_exhausted` or `cap_reached`; in shadow mode the verdict is recorded (`schedule_shadow_would_skip`) and the run starts; contracts are never checked. An admission refusal that races the check skips the same way. DST and catch-up rules are unchanged.
- **Skip alerts** (`skip-alerts.ts`): one in-app notice `schedule_skipped` (billing category: owners, editors, operators) and one branded EN/ES `schedule_skipped` email per owner who keeps billing email on, claimed per dispatch in `billing.alert_state` and retried by later ticks (three attempts). Stripe test mode emails only Caudals' addresses.
- **Runs charge through L-11's path** (`createSelfServiceRun` → admission, holds, captures, meters with the run id). The billing watch also pauses a scheduled run once its schedule's runs reach the limit (`cap_reached`, alert `cap_reached` scope `schedule`, once per schedule and month).
- **Regression summary after each scheduled run** (`alerts.ts`, `summary.ts`, `destinations.ts`): classification unchanged; the alert now waits for the run's report (or 30 minutes) so links open it, and stores `evidence.summary` (both scores, up to five newly broken questions most severe first, fixed count, `comparable`). It goes to every destination in the alert's transaction: the in-app notice `monitor_<status>` (score against last time, "not comparable", newly broken questions with links in the notification centre), the signed webhook (payload unchanged), and **`registerSummaryDestination` for L-19's Slack/Teams**. A `monitor_regression` notice is emailed (`monitor_regression` template, EN/ES) once per person to members who keep **Regressions** on; runs that cannot be compared are `inconclusive` and never alert. `GET /alerts` returns the summary; Settings → Monitoring shows the score against last time and the newly broken count.
- **Run again and compare** (`run-comparison.ts`, `GET /api/evals/v1/runs/:id/comparison[?baselineRunId&focus]`, read-only; `components/evals/run-comparison.tsx`): results show **Run again** (the frozen test set, after the L-15 estimate where billing shows) and **Compare** (pick an earlier run; still records the comparison through `POST /comparisons`); from the second run the comparison with the previous run (improved / regressed / unchanged, score against last time, questions that moved, each opening its answer, "not comparable" with reasons) **opens on its own** on the results and at the top of the report's Overview, and scrolls into view after a run started from the page.
- **L-17's "Re-run these questions"** is enabled for owners and editors: it opens the evaluation with `?rerun=1&focus=<case ids>`, which asks first (the estimate dialog, or a confirmation without billing; never on arrival, and `rerun` leaves the address), runs the whole approved test set again so scores stay comparable, and lists the fix's questions first in the comparison.
- **Schedule dialog** on credits: weekly/monthly only, the estimate per run and per month (`monthlyProjection`: monthly 1 run, weekly 4–5, daily 28–31), balance, the owner's credit limit with a warning below one run's estimate. Help article "Schedules and regression alerts" (EN/ES) updated.
- **Fix found in production (3cdb6e88):** `billingEmailAllowed` read only `COLLABORATION_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL`, but the evals workers mount it as a `_FILE` secret, so the scheduler skipped every test-mode billing alert to the internal address (L-15's alerts too). It now reads the variable or its file.
