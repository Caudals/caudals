## What shipped

- **Console** `app.caudals.com/ops/platform/billing` (Platform → Billing; `components/evals/ops-billing*.tsx`): account search and account view (ledger timeline, lots, holds, orders, invoices and rectifying invoices, alerts, approvals, override, the §6.9 invariants; opening an account is an audited support read), Approvals, Price book, Reconciliation, Manual invoices (L-13), Monthly export (L-13), Fraud queue, per-workspace overrides (L-14). Also: credits per workspace in Platform · Usage, credits held and charged per attempt in the run inspector, the €0 warning links to the price book, Clients filters pay-as-you-go workspaces. EN/ES, 390/768/1440 px.
- **Actions** (`lib/billing/ops.ts`, `/api/evals/v1/billing/admin/{grants,adjustments,credit-refunds,refunds,expiry,freeze}`): grant (amount, reason, expiry, campaign tag), adjust ±, refund credits (a run's charges or one entry), refund money for an untouched purchase within 14 days (L-12's ledger-then-Stripe path), extend a lot's expiry, freeze/unfreeze. Platform admins only, a sign-in within 15 minutes, a reason, an `Idempotency-Key`, the ledger entry and an audit event in one transaction.
- **Two-person rule** (migration 094, `billing.approval_request`): above 10,000 credits (€100), or a money refund above €100 net, the action becomes a request (payload and its SHA-256, requester, 24-hour expiry). A different platform admin approves the hash they reviewed and the action runs as them; the requester cancels, another admin rejects. Backstop: the `ledger_entry_two_person` trigger refuses any large person-written credit entry without an approval that person approved.
- **Price notices** (`lib/billing/price-notice.ts`): a revision that raises any rate takes effect only if its notice went out at least 30 days before its date; otherwise the previous revision stays in effect. Recipients are the owners of credit workspaces that used a raised model in the last 90 days. `BILLING_PRICE_NOTICES` decides who gets the email (below).
- **Daily reconciliation** (`lib/billing/reconcile.ts`, evals scheduler at 05:00 UTC for the previous UTC day and at every scheduler start; `POST /admin/reconciliation` on demand): Stripe balance transactions ↔ orders ↔ ledger purchases, bonuses, refunds and chargebacks; invoices and rectifying invoices against orders; holds open over 25 hours; account invariants; the L-11 shadow diff. Stored append-only in `billing.reconciliation_report`; the console shows the clean-day streak (Gate B needs three). Alerts `billing_reconciliation` and `price_notice_due` in `scripts/evals/health-alerts.sh` (runbook 23).
- Docs: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (Billing: Ops Console, Two-person Approvals and Reconciliation), `docs/TOOLS.md` (Ops Billing Console and Daily Reconciliation), `docs/evals/runbooks.md` (23).

## Risks and follow-ups

- **Production write paths are unverified in production** (by the founder's stop): grant, adjust, reversal, run refund, money refund, approvals, freeze, expiry and notices ran only on the disposable database with the same migration (checksum-identical) and the UI harness. Founder action 3 closes the core of it.
- **Shadow-admission artefact (engineering follow-up, L-11 code).** Switching a workspace's enforcement override back to `inherit` within 5 minutes of a run admitted under `enforce` gives that run a shadow admission, and the shadow diff then counts its real-ledger meters as missing shadow meters (in the shadow report and in the reconciliation). Fix: skip runs that already have real ledger holds or meters in `runShadowAdmissions` (or record the admission mode at admission), or let `success_without_action` accept a real `unit:` meter. Until then, avoid override switches around verification runs.
- The page answers an unauthenticated request with a streamed redirect (HTTP 200 carrying `NEXT_REDIRECT` and a meta refresh) rather than a 307 header; the body has no console data, and the APIs answer 401.
- Reconciliation covers the configured Stripe mode only; test-mode reports say nothing about live money.
- Test-mode documents waiting for the accountant (3 invoices, 3 rectifying invoices, `pending_issue`) appear in the test-mode export only; they are not reconciliation differences.
