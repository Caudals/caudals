## What shipped

1. **Billing overflow at 390 px (high).** `.p-billing` was a one-column grid whose implicit column grew to the min-content width of the pill tabs (`width: max-content`) and the stat grid, so the canvas scrolled sideways by 146–203 px on every view. It is now `minmax(0, 1fr)`; the pill tabs shrink and scroll inside their track with the "?" on the same line (it wrapped to its own row and sat 11 px low on desktop), and the balance takes the first row of the stat card on phones.
2. **React #418 on English help pages (high), root cause: Cloudflare email obfuscation.** Diffing the served HTML with the hydrated DOM showed `Email [email protected]` and `<a class="__cf_email__">` where React expected `Email hello@caudals.com`: the zone rewrites every address in the HTML and adds a decoder script; when React hydrated before the decoder ran, text mismatched (a race, so intermittent; Spanish pages render in the browser, so English only). Addresses in help now render through `EmailText` (inside Cloudflare's documented `<!--email_off-->` markers, as the public `EmailLink` does since L-24d) and `mailto:` hrefs are set once the page is interactive. Bare addresses in articles become mailto links.
3. **Console 404s from gated probes (high).** `GET /billing/summary` (every page, every member of a workspace without billing), `GET /billing/plan` (new evaluation, limit dialog) and `GET /engine/byok` (every owner Settings tab, ⌘K) answered 404 where the feature is off. They now answer 200 `null` to the people who would otherwise see the feature (billing: owners and editors; plan: members; own keys: owners and platform admins), and 404 to everyone else; every other billing and BYOK route still answers 404. The shell does not poll a `null` billing answer.
4. **Billing polish.** Top up and Billing details are hidden while buying is closed (each only said "not available", Billing details with the wrong message); the Overview says it once and drops the auto-recharge row nobody could turn on. Usage: "No usage in this period." instead of an empty plot; Period select and Export CSV aligned; Returned, Quantity, Change and invoice Total right-aligned (`DataTable` `align: "num"`); account activity with exact dates instead of "26 minutes ago". English percentages without the Spanish space ("+20% on your first top-up").
5. **Settings polish.** Plan and limits singulars ("1 run at a time", "Up to 1 AI system", "1 of 1 AI system in use"; ES "1 ejecución a la vez"…). Selected tab kept in view in any scrolling tab row (Settings tabs at 390 hid "Developers" when selected): R4's `TabStrip` landed first and does it; R3 adds a second trigger when a tab arrives later (Billing → Invoices shifted "Limits and alerts" half out of the track). No double rule under the tabs. Notification checkbox lists share one left edge on wide screens. Invite dialog says "to this workspace" instead of the ops word "client". "Test set and results ready". The workspace name to type in the deletion dialog is bold. Model credit rates read "Credits per 1M tokens: 50 in · 200 out".
6. **Help polish.** Article pages read in a 70ch column (were ~110 characters a line), without the in-page "All articles" link (DESIGN §9: the breadcrumb is the way back), with "Reviewed 12 October 2026" (day first like every other app date), no 56 px gap under the summary, and list titles aligned with the search box.
