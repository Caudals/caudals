## What shipped

- **Gating** (`lib/billing/billing-ui.ts`): new server flag `BILLING_UI_ENABLED` (default off; allow-listed in `scripts/set-app-credentials.sh`; copied to the scheduler). Every customer billing surface shows only when `BILLING_UI_ENABLED=true` **and** `BILLING_CREDITS_ENABLED=true` **and** the workspace is eligible: `PLANS_V2_ENABLED` (Gate B), or its admin override `plans_v2` or `live_payments` (L-14), or it is in `BILLING_TEST_WORKSPACE_IDS`. Before Gate B no real customer sees balances or top-ups; design partners get it through the `livePayments`/`plansV2` override. Within a workspace: owners everything, editors and Caudals operators the balance and usage, viewers nothing (404 server-side; the shell never asks for viewers). Platform admins read for support (audited).
- **APIs** (`lib/billing/customer-api.ts`): `GET /billing/summary`, `GET /billing/usage[?format=csv]`, `GET /billing/ledger?cursor=` (owners), `GET/PUT /billing/caps` (PUT owners, Idempotency-Key, audited), `GET /billing/rates`. `POST /billing/estimates` now also serves editors of eligible workspaces while `BILLING_ESTIMATES_ENABLED=operators`, and returns the run gate.
- **Every number from the ledger** (`lib/billing/account-view.ts`): usage = debit entries minus the entries reversing them; balances = the verified account row. Tested against independent SQL sums.
- **Settings → Billing and usage**: Overview, Top up (packs, VAT preview, first-top-up badge, Checkout, auto-recharge settings, saved card), Usage (day chart with table, by evaluation and run linking to the report or evaluation, by model and meter, CSV, owners' account activity), Invoices (shown when L-13's API answers; `BILLING_INVOICING_PROVIDER=manual` is on, so it shows), Billing details (VIES status, Customer Portal), Limits and alerts. Sidebar plan-and-balance pill; low-balance banner; Settings → General "Plan and limits" (machine spend and entitlement amendments stay in ops); Home's plan card shows the balance; ⌘K lists the tab; contextual "?" to the updated help article.
- **Run estimate dialog**: every run started where billing shows (Run evaluation, Run again, Approve and run) opens it: questions, connection, models with credit rates, p50–p90 credits with euros, balance, largest hold, time range and the §6.7 copy. Blocks with "Add X credits" where credits are enforced and the balance is below p90, unless auto-recharge covers the gap; contract workspaces have no gate. Owners get the one-step top-up (covering pack preselected); editors are told to ask an owner.
- **Paused for credits**: "Paused — add credits to continue" with Add credits, Resume and Finish; a cap pause links owners to Limits. A paid top-up or auto-recharge now **resumes work paused for `credits_exhausted` by itself** (`resumeAfterCredits`), never a cap pause.
- **Caps** (`lib/billing/limits.ts`, `lib/billing/watch.ts`): monthly cap (usage plus holds) and per-run cap (2 × p90 by default), enforced where credits are enforced: at admission (`409 CAP_REACHED`) and by the scheduler's billing watch, which pauses runs with `cap_reached`. `admitRun` also admits a shortfall that auto-recharge covers.
- **Alerts** (billing watch, only where billing shows and the account ever had credits): low balance (once per refill), cap 50/80/100 %, credits expiring in 30/7 days, auto-recharge succeeded, unusual spend (in the app only); notices (category `billing`, viewers never, payment notices owners only) plus L-07 emails to owners with billing email on. Test mode emails only Caudals' own addresses.
- **Model rates**: Evaluation · Connect shows customers the models a run uses with their credit rates, and the price list on pay as you go. Choosing a commercial model stays an ops action (D-09: content goes to a commercial provider only on opt-in); platform admins change models from the dialog.
- **Migration 109 `evals_lifecycle_payg`**: the L-07 lifecycle cohort includes pay-as-you-go workspaces (L-14 follow-up).

## Risks and notes

- Caps are enforced by the scheduler (≤ 1 minute late) and at admission: worker roles cannot read `billing.spend_cap`. Overshoot is bounded by the calls in flight in that minute. Per-schedule caps are L-18's; grading and report passes already started are not stopped by a cap.
- The low-balance threshold is the §6.10 default (500 credits or 20 % of the last pack): a configurable threshold needs a column (follow-up).
- Customers cannot pick commercial models themselves (D-09 opt-in); the price list and rates are shown, and ops switch models.
- `RunPipeline` upcoming stages fail axe colour contrast (pre-existing; excluded in the paused-state axe check; L-24).
- A purchase does not reopen a run paused at a cap; resuming a cap pause re-checks the caps at admission.
- The first test sign-up showed that the synthetic fixture rejects `max_tokens` above 500: API systems created in the app use 4,000, so the check recreated the system through the API with a 500-token limit.
