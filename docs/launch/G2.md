## What shipped

1. **Provider-price alert (L-10).** The weekly check only logged `billing_price_check_changes`. Now:
   - `billing.price_check_ack` (append-only) records a platform admin's acknowledgement of a check's findings, with a note; `billing.price_check_status()` reports the latest check, the rates that need ops (listed price moved, or cost past the 3 % recompute threshold) and the acknowledgement that covers them. The acknowledgement covers a fingerprint of the findings (revision + each alerting rate with its listed price and flags), so next week's identical findings stay acknowledged and anything new alerts again.
   - `scripts/evals/health-alerts.sh` opens `price_check_changes` while the latest check has unacknowledged findings (runbook 24, drill key included).
   - Console → Price book → *Provider-price check* panel (rates, book → listed, recompute) and *Acknowledge* (recent sign-in, `Idempotency-Key`, audited `billing.price_check_acknowledged`); `POST /api/evals/v1/billing/admin/price-book/checks/:id/acknowledge`.
2. **Shadow admissions on resume (L-11).** A resume of a run admitted in shadow mode now records `admission:<run>:resume:<workflow event id>` for the p90 of the units left, the amount enforce mode's `admitRun` checks at resume, in the resume's transaction and its own savepoint (`recordShadowResumeAdmission`, never raises). The L-16b admission-mode model decides: runs admitted under enforce (frozen mode, else their charges) get none. The shadow report's admission totals and `admissions_blocked` now include resumes.
3. **Manual tax review (L-12).** Console → *Tax reviews*: profiles waiting for review (VIES unavailable; Canary Islands, Ceuta, Melilla), oldest first, with the purchases refused since (checkout attempts, recorded by `billing.record_blocked_top_up`, and auto-recharges) and open orders; *Review* sets the treatment for the version reviewed (`billing.resolve_tax_review`, recent sign-in, audited `billing.tax_review_resolved` with before/after). The database enforces §6.14:
   - Canary Islands, Ceuta, Melilla (outside the IVA territory, art. 3 LIVA): `non_eu_not_subject` ("Operación no sujeta"; the customer accounts for IGIC/IPSI; B2B services located where the customer is established, art. 69.Uno.1º) or `es_vat` when the establishment receiving the service is in the IVA territory. Never a reverse charge (not an intra-EU supply).
   - VIES unavailable: `eu_reverse_charge` only with the VIES consultation number the reviewer obtained (status `valid`), else `es_vat` (status stays `unavailable`; the purchase-time recheck keeps the reviewed IVA while VIES is down and applies VIES's answer when it returns).
   - Re-saving billing details re-decides the treatment (a special territory goes back to review).
   The ops label for `non_eu_not_subject` is now "Not subject to Spanish IVA" (and in the accountant export "Not subject to Spanish IVA (outside the IVA territory)"), true for both non-EU and Canary/Ceuta/Melilla customers.
4. **Unusual-spend email (L-15).** The billing watch emails `unusual_spend` (L-07 template, EN/ES, billing footer) with the in-app notice: owners who keep billing email on (`evals.notification_email_recipients` gains the kind), once per UTC day (`billing.alert_state`), test mode only to Caudals addresses. Sample sends: `scripts/evals/send-sample-email.ts unusual_spend <en|es>`.
5. **Low-balance threshold (L-15).** `billing.alert_setting` + `billing.set_low_balance_threshold` (owners, admins, services; whole credits; audited). Settings → Billing and usage → Limits and alerts: *Default: N credits* or *My own threshold*, saved with the caps (`PUT /billing/caps` `lowBalanceThresholdCredits`, null = default, omitted = unchanged). No row = today's rule (500 credits or 20 % of the last pack). The banner, pill and alert use it.
6. **Paused-run contrast (L-15).** The pipeline's stages not reached yet use `--p-text-secondary` (tertiary was the weakest tier: 4.6:1 in light after L-24, 3.7:1 under the authored dark theme); the current stage stands out by weight, count and bar. `e2e/evals/billing.contract.ts` no longer excludes `.p-pipeline` and also runs axe on it at 390 px.
