## 2. What changed

**Phone layout (390 px)**

- **Report heading** (functional #2): `.p-head-actions` is never wider than its heading (`max-width: 100%`), so a long action row (Present · Ask this report · Share · Download · ⋯) wraps instead of widening the canvas by 132 px (EN) or 209 px (ES). On phones a report's actions sit under its title, so the title and description get the full width. This is a kit rule, so every page with a long action row benefits.
- **Result evidence view** (functional #3): the result navigation wraps on phones. Back and previous/next are on the first line, the position and "Copy question" on the second.
- **Results and the reports list**: a new kit option, `DataTable stack`. Under 640 px each row becomes a block instead of a table that scrolls sideways with clipped columns. A `data-cell="menu"` cell sits beside the title, a `data-cell="wide"` cell goes last, and `data-label` prefixes a value ("Severity: High").
- **Shared-view preview**: `.p-panel-body` holds one `minmax(0, 1fr)` column. The panel's grid column had sized itself to the report's tabs and pushed the preview 25 px off a phone.
- **Topic and severity bars**: on phones the bar takes the full width under its label and value.

**Report content**

- **Verdict first.** The Overview now runs: executive summary → limitations → score → comparison with the previous run (or the example's trend) → Fix these first. Before, the score sat about 1,000 px down at 1440 and about 2,600 px down on a phone. The L-17 rule "summary above the scoreboard" holds.
- **Withdrawn and draft reports.** A withdrawn report (7 in production, reachable by link) rendered as a valid report with a readout booking and share. It now opens with a warn notice and a "Withdrawn" badge, and offers no next steps, Present, Ask or Share. A draft shows an info notice and a "Draft" badge, and its Share step is hidden (the API refuses to share a draft).
- **Plurals** in the shared summary and readout copy (web, PDF, Word and slides change together): "Sample: 1 scored answer out of 1 question", "1 question from your sources", and the Spanish equivalents.
- **Comparison without a score**: "No score in this run; was 100%." replaces "Score —, was 100%.".
- **Spanish**:
  - Topics read as words, by the PDF's rule. Before, the web showed raw codes such as "franquicias_y_limites".
  - Rates in the score card and Methodology use the decimal comma and spaced sign ("76,1 %"), with "Intervalo del 95 %".
  - "Waiting for the answer judge." is translated in the row and the evidence view (functional #11), through R4's `engineText()`.
  - Language codes show as names.
- **Methodology**:
  - Sentence values sit in a left-aligned `DefinitionList layout="prose"`.
  - The "Evaluated" times render after hydration, which fixes React #418 on the example's Methodology tab (functional #5, example part; the help pages are R3's).
- **Billing limitation in plain English.** The deployed-system limitation that every incomplete report shows first now reads "Your provider bills you for the calls this test made to your system. Caudals doesn't know your rates or the total, and the run's call and token limits don't guarantee a spending cap."
  - It is applied by `localizeReportText`, so the web, the PDF and Word agree.
  - Snapshots keep the original sentence.
  - The Spanish sentence was rewritten to match.

**Readout**

- Review-status chips on slides use the app's tones. "Preliminary" is info (it was an amber warning), and "Expert review" is pass (it was amber).
- Presenter notes are at title size (20 px).
- Under 700 px the keyboard key list is hidden, so the notes are no longer pushed off screen.
- An editor's "not available for your role" notice is info, with "Back to the report" instead of "Exit".

**Shared with R1 and R4 (one implementation each).** R1 merged the same `DataTable stack` block, and R2 uses it for the results and the Reports list. R4 merged the phone rule that puts heading actions under the title and the `minmax(0, 1fr)` side-panel column, so R2's duplicates were dropped in the rebase. R2's `.p-head-actions` max-width remains: it is what stops the overflow.

## 4. Production verification

Production verification, read-only, with the QA login (its own saved session; the QA login now holds `platform_admin`, so the operator actions show on every report). Captures are in `~/.cache/caudals-qa/after/R2/`, made with the same capture script: 31 screen states (21 customer-pass, 8 operator and readout, 2 signed out), 240 files at 390, 768 and 1440 px in EN and ES.

- **Horizontal overflow: 0 states.** Before: 10. Those were the operator dialogs' page at 132 px (EN) and 209 px (ES), and the Spanish result view at 79 px, in both the report and the example.
- **Errors:** no page errors and no React #418 on the example's Methodology tab. The browser guard blocked 0 writes, no question was asked, and every dialog was closed unconfirmed.
- **React #418 on the example's Methodology tab: gone.** The cause was the "Evaluated" times: the server renders in UTC (the app has no `TZ`), the browser in the reader's zone (09:00 against 11:00 for Madrid). No email address is involved, so it is a different cause from R3's Cloudflare one, and the served HTML has no `__cf_email__`. Now the server sends "5 Oct 2026 – 5 Oct 2026" and the browser shows "5 Oct 2026, 11:00 – 5 Oct 2026, 11:36" after hydration. Probe (`after/R2/scripts/probe-418.cjs`, read-only, en-GB, Europe/Madrid): Methodology EN 0 of 25 loads with a hydration error, ES 0 of 10, Overview EN 0 of 10, Test results EN 0 of 10.
- **Heading:** at 390 px it wraps to "Presentar · Pregunta a tu informe" over "Descargar · ⋯" under the title.
- **Result view:** reads "Esperando al juez de respuestas.", and its navigation fits.
- **Withdrawn report:** the warn notice and "Withdrawn" badge show, with no next steps, Share, Present or Ask.
- **Reports list and share preview:** the list stacks at 390 px, and the shared-view preview fits its panel.
- **Example:** Overview shows the score before the trend, and the topics read "Franquicias y límites", "Cambios y bajas", "Fuera de alcance".
- **Readout:** the cover slide reads "1 question from your sources" with a blue "Preliminary" chip. The phone presenter view shows the notes at 20 px without the key list.
- **Not checked in production:** the PDF and Word wording (the plain billing limitation and the singular sample line). Producing a file is an export, which is a write. `report-pdf`, `readout-pdf` and `report-cross-format` cover them, and the documents worker runs `dbd095ae`.
