## Design (decided while building)

- Partner members reach client workspaces through ordinary membership rows marked with `membership.partner_id`, kept in step by one SECURITY DEFINER sync (`evals._partner_sync`). Every existing RLS policy, `requireWorkspace` and the workspace switcher work unchanged; removing a member, unlinking a client, suspending or closing the partner deletes those rows in the same transaction (audited). Partner people get customer roles only (`owner` for partner admins on clients the partner created, `editor` otherwise), never the workspace `operator` role, so they never count as platform staff (no readout notes, operator estimates, recipe authority, `/ops`).
- Referral codes `cp-<10 hex>` on `?partner=` / `?ref=` links travel through L-02's first touch (consented cookie) → `workspace.acquisition_attribution` → first paid order. Triggers on `evals.workspace` and `billing.top_up_order` record `evals.partner_referral` and its first payment; failures there never block a sign-up or a payment.
- Referred revenue is computed from `billing.top_up_order` (first year from the first payment, refunds and chargebacks excluded); `null` until a paid order exists.
- Partner allowance: an agreement row (credits per Madrid month) and ops grants into a client workspace through `billing.grant` (source `contract_allowance`, ref `partner`), capped by the month's allowance.
- White-label logo: PNG/JPEG ≤ 512 KB in private storage (`evals/partners/<id>/logo/<uuid>`); PDF export jobs carry the brand (`export_job.brand`) chosen by the app; the documents worker re-checks the link before reading the logo. Branded files are stored under `.b<sha8>` so a logo change renders a fresh copy.
