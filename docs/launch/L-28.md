## What shipped

- **Migration 103 `evals_partners`.** `evals.partner` (status `active`/`suspended`/`closed`, revenue share in basis points, 20 % by default, client cap, optional logo reference), `partner_member` (`admin` | `member`), `partner_client` (one live link per workspace; `created` by the partner with access `manage`, or `linked` by ops), `partner_referral_code` (`cp-` + 10 hex, unique, revocable), `partner_referral` (one referring partner per workspace, with its first paid order), `partner_allowance` and `partner_allowance_grant`; `membership.partner_id`; `export_job.brand`. RLS: partner members read only their own partner's rows, platform admins all, a client's own members see their workspace's link; the runtime has SELECT only. Every write is a SECURITY DEFINER function that checks the actor in the database and is audited. 093's member-cap functions now skip partner memberships. The documents worker gets only `evals.partner_pdf_logo`. Rollback refuses once a partner, a partner membership or a branded export exists.
- **Operator client model scoped to the partner.** Partner people reach clients through ordinary membership rows marked with the partner, kept in step by `evals._partner_sync` in the same transaction as every change (person added or removed, client linked or unlinked, partner suspended or closed). Partner admins are `owner` of clients the partner created, everyone else `editor`; never the workspace `operator` role, so no platform-operator power anywhere. A person's own membership is never touched. Unlinking, closing or removing the last admin is refused while a managed client would be left without an owner of its own.
- **Referral codes through to payment.** Links `caudals.com/{en,es}/demo?…&partner=<code>` and `app.caudals.com/workspace/start?…&partner=<code>`; the code rides L-02's consented first touch to the demo run, the sign-up and `workspace.acquisition_attribution`. A trigger on `evals.workspace` attributes the workspace (`partner`, then `ref`, against a live code of an active partner); a trigger on `billing.top_up_order` records its first paid order (or attributes then, for a code issued after sign-up). Both triggers swallow their own errors: attribution never fails a sign-up or a payment. Revocation stops new attributions only.
- **Partner dashboard** `app.caudals.com/partner` (EN/ES, platform kit): stats, clients with activity (last activity, evaluations, runs this month, latest score, credits), referral links with copy buttons, referred accounts with first-year revenue (null until they pay, never an invented 0), the allowance, the monthly statement CSV and the report logo; Partner group in the sidebar. Copy in `lib/evals/partners/copy.ts`.
- **Statement** `GET /api/evals/v1/partners/:id/statement?month=&mode=`: first-year referred payments and their refunds/chargebacks with the share, allowance grants (ledger amounts) and each client's credit use while linked, for a Madrid month and one Stripe mode, with totals and the credit equivalent (100 credits per euro). Payouts stay manual.
- **Allowance:** ops grant from the agreement's monthly credits into a live client through `billing.grant` (source `contract_allowance`, ref `partner`), never above the month (idempotent per key).
- **White-label PDF:** PNG/JPEG logo (≤ 512 KB, 16–4,096 px, checked from the bytes) in private storage; the app stores the brand on the PDF job, the documents worker re-checks the live link and logo hash before reading it; the cover keeps the Caudals brand bar and adds "Delivered by <partner>" with the logo and "Evaluation and methodology by Caudals". Branded files end `.b<sha8>.pdf`, so a logo change renders a fresh copy. Share PDFs, Word and readout decks stay unbranded.
- **Ops API** (platform admins, recent sign-in, reason, audited): partners, people, client links, allowance and grants (`docs/TOOLS.md` → Partner Accounts has the hand-onboarding steps).
- Docs: ARCHITECTURE (Partner Accounts and Referral Codes), FRONTEND, DESIGN, TOOLS, `docs/evals/AGENTS.md`, `AGENTS.md`.

## Risks and notes

- Cross-tenant exposure is bounded by memberships: every partner path is checked in SQL; RLS hides partner rows; partner people never get the operator role. A client's own owner cannot remove partner people (member management is admin-only, as for every workspace); ops unlink.
- Partner memberships survive in deleted workspaces (tombstones, as the deletion workflow leaves them); they grant nothing there.
- A person who accepts an invitation into a workspace where they already hold a partner membership keeps the partner membership (removed on unlink).
- The full harness run once failed L-22's workspace-home "needs action" contract; it passed 19/19 on rerun (timing).
