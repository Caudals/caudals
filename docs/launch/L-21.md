## What shipped

- **`/ops/prospects`** (list, `/new`, `/:id`; `components/evals/prospect-snapshot.tsx`), for platform operators and admins only, behind the server-read flag `PROSPECT_SNAPSHOTS_ENABLED`. With the flag off, pages and APIs answer 404 and the nav entry is hidden. The form asks for company, public chat URL, help-centre URL, language and testing basis (public information, or owner permission with a required note), plus one policy confirmation. That is well under two minutes of input. The third-party policy is shown in full.
- **Sandbox and basis record** (migration 090, `evals.create_prospect_snapshot`). One SECURITY DEFINER call:
  - creates the sandbox workspace;
  - sets its entitlement: one website system, 10 tests by default, 3 runs a month, one active run, no schedules;
  - writes `evals.prospect_snapshot`: company, target and help hosts, basis, approved scope, traffic limits, creator and Madrid day.

  The rest of the setup is idempotent: the project, the website system (4 requests a minute, one session, preselected for runs), an `authorization_record` of basis `external_snapshot`, the queued connection check, the evaluation, and the help centre as a 10-page website source. `POST /prospects/:id/setup` finishes a setup that stopped part-way.
- **Limits**:
  - a daily cap per Europe/Madrid day (`PROSPECT_SNAPSHOT_DAILY_CAP`, default 25, the §7.5 Monday volume), counted in `evals.prospect_snapshot_day`;
  - at most one snapshot per chat host in 24 hours.
- **Policy enforcement**:
  - The API refuses any question pack other than the ordinary `generic-grounded-qa`, naming adversarial packs explicitly (prompt injection, jailbreak, prompt extraction, red team). It also refuses non-public URLs: http, ports, credentials, IP addresses and private names.
  - The database refuses a sandbox run unless it has a website target within the recorded rate and one session, and a frozen set of `grounded_qa` cases with no adversarial tags and no more questions than the snapshot allows. The error is `SNAPSHOT_POLICY_BLOCKED`, answered as 422.
  - The `external_snapshot` basis exists only in a sandbox. Assistance (sign-in, CAPTCHA, unverified completion) is never bypassed.
- **Private link**: one click makes a bearer share of the current published report. A trigger on `share_grant` labels it `external_snapshot` (new nullable column), clamps its expiry to 30 days and refuses any other audience in a sandbox. A new link revokes the old one, and the token is shown once and never stored. `/share` shows an "External snapshot" notice with the scope and the expiry date (EN/ES; exchange `meta.share`).
- **Outreach summary**: the §7.5 templates (LinkedIn first; email only with a documented legal basis, with identity and opt-out lines), filled with the snapshot's counts and leading finding in EN and ES, ready to edit. They include the scope line that evals spec §16.5 requires. Weak evidence (fewer than five scored answers, or nothing to fix) is flagged "don't send". Auto-graded reports are labelled as such.
- **Create CRM draft**: an explicit click writes an immutable `evals.crm_handoff_draft` (kind `external_snapshot`, destination `warmbly`, `sent: false`, the edited outreach with link tokens scrubbed, tags) and shows a block to paste into Warmbly. `/ops/growth` has the same per account (kind `account`, from the latest published report). Nothing calls Warmbly or sends anything.
- **Weekly digest** (`lib/evals/operations/ops-digest.ts`, evals scheduler, `OPS_DIGEST_ENABLED`): sent Mondays from 08:00 Europe/Madrid (DST-safe), only to the internal `COLLABORATION_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL`, which is mounted as the versioned secret `caudals_evals_digest_to_<hash>`.
  - It covers last week's funnel against the §7.11 targets (`GROWTH_TARGET_STAGE`, Gate A by default), evidence snapshots, stuck runs (no progress for 2 hours), failed demos by reason, credit holds older than 24 hours, low-balance accounts and new sign-ups to call (company, email domain, source, stage).
  - It sends once per week: a database claim, retried after 15 minutes up to five attempts, plus a Resend idempotency key per week. `evals.ops_digest_send` keeps counts only.
  - Manual run: `services/evals-worker/ops-digest.ts --dry-run | --force` (docs/TOOLS.md).
- **Growth view**: new accounts show company, email domain, connected system, stage, last activity, latest score and credits bought. Credits bought shows `—` until a purchase exists, never an invented 0. There is also "Create CRM draft". Prospect sandboxes are excluded from every count. Details come from `evals.ops_workspace_details()` (staff only; admins see every workspace, operators only their memberships).
- **Overview**: growth KPIs for the last 7 days against targets (sign-ups, activation, paying customers, snapshots shared) and demo health (starts, success, p50, queue, model routes, failure reasons, stuck runs).
- **Clients**: plan, credits (and credits bought), email domain and last activity, with filters All, Managed, Free and Snapshot. Search also matches domains.
- **Review queue**: waiting-time badges (due at 24 h, overdue at 48 h) on test sets and results, and bulk approve with one reason (one attributed decision per result).

## Not done (and why)

- **"Assign to me"** in the Review queue needs an assignment model and multi-operator rules. Today there are no platform operators and two admins. Left for a later package.
- **Direct Warmbly API push**: the app holds no Warmbly credential, and the draft must never send. The CRM draft is a database record the operator copies into Warmbly. Pushing it would need a scoped key and an import adapter (founder decision, §5).
- **System type from onboarding answers**: L-06 adds the welcome answers to `/ops/growth`. This package shows the connected system kind.
- **Help-centre rights**: the source rights enum has no "public third-party" value. The help centre is stored as `customer_owned`, which keeps public release blocked; the real basis is on the snapshot record.

## Migration 090 `evals_prospect_snapshots`

The migration is additive:

- **Tables**: `evals.prospect_snapshot`, `evals.prospect_snapshot_day` and `evals.ops_digest_send`, all with staff-only SELECT RLS. Operators may update only the link columns of a snapshot; nobody can insert, delete or update its basis directly.
- **Column**: `share_grant.label`.
- **Triggers**: three, on `authorization_record`, `run` and `share_grant`.
- **SECURITY DEFINER functions**: `create_prospect_snapshot`, `ops_workspace_details`, `ops_digest_data`, `claim_ops_digest`, `finish_ops_digest` and `is_platform_staff`. `prospect_sandbox` is trigger-only and not granted.

The rollback refuses while any snapshot, labelled share or digest send exists (tested). The migration was applied in production on 2026-10-12 at about 20:15 UTC with `scripts/launch/migrate-production.sh` (dry run first). Catalog check: `evals_runtime` can execute `create_prospect_snapshot` but not `prospect_sandbox`.

## Risks and notes

- The list badge shows the link's expiry date. It cannot see a revocation, because shares live in each sandbox. The detail page shows the real state.
- The scheduler restart path depends on `COLLABORATION_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL` being present in the app credentials, because `deploy-evals-production.sh` now requires it, like the Resend keys.
- Snapshots are legally sensitive (R3): ordinary questions, public information, private links and no public naming are enforced. Sending outreach still needs the founder's legal basis (D-13).
