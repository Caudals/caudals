## What shipped

- **`/ops/prospects`** (list, `/new`, `/:id`; `components/evals/prospect-snapshot.tsx`), platform operators and admins only, behind the server-read flag `PROSPECT_SNAPSHOTS_ENABLED` (pages and APIs 404 otherwise; nav entry only when on). The form takes company, public chat URL, help-centre URL, language, testing basis (public information / owner permission with a required note) and one policy confirmation: well under two minutes of input. It shows the third-party policy in full.
- **Sandbox and basis record** (migration 090, `evals.create_prospect_snapshot`): one SECURITY DEFINER call creates the sandbox workspace, sets its entitlement (one website system, 10 tests by default, 3 runs a month, one active run, no schedules) and writes `evals.prospect_snapshot` (company, target and help hosts, basis, approved scope, traffic limits, creator, Madrid day). Then, idempotently: project, website system at 4 requests a minute and one session (preselected in the evaluation), an `authorization_record` of basis `external_snapshot`, the queued connection check, the evaluation and the help centre as a 10-page website source. A retry finishes a part-way setup (`POST /prospects/:id/setup`).
- **Limits**: daily cap per Europe/Madrid day (`PROSPECT_SNAPSHOT_DAILY_CAP`, default 25, the §7.5 Monday volume) counted in `evals.prospect_snapshot_day`; at most one snapshot per chat host in 24 hours.
- **Policy enforcement**: the API blocks any question pack other than the ordinary `generic-grounded-qa` (adversarial names such as prompt injection, jailbreak, prompt extraction, red team are rejected explicitly) and non-public URLs (http, ports, credentials, IPs, private names). In the database, a sandbox run needs a website target within the recorded rate and one session, and a frozen set of `grounded_qa` cases without adversarial tags and no larger than the snapshot allows; the `external_snapshot` basis exists only in a sandbox. Assistance (CAPTCHA, sign-in) is never bypassed.
- **Private link**: one click makes a bearer share of the current published report, labelled `external_snapshot` and clamped to 30 days by a trigger on `share_grant` (which also refuses other audiences in a sandbox); a new link revokes the old one; the token is shown once. The `/share` page shows an "External snapshot" notice with the scope and the expiry date (EN/ES).
- **Outreach summary**: the §7.5 templates (LinkedIn first, email only with a documented legal basis; identity and opt-out lines) filled with the snapshot's counts and leading finding, in EN and ES, editable, with the scope line required by evals spec §16.5. Weak evidence (under five scored answers, or nothing to fix) is flagged "don't send"; auto-graded reports say so.
- **Create CRM draft**: an explicit click writes an immutable `evals.crm_handoff_draft` (kind `external_snapshot`, destination `warmbly`, `sent: false`, the edited outreach with link tokens scrubbed, tags) and shows a block to paste into Warmbly. The growth view gets the same per account (kind `account`, from the latest published report). Nothing calls Warmbly or sends anything.
- **Weekly digest**: the scheduler sends one plain-text email every Monday from 08:00 Madrid (DST-safe) when `OPS_DIGEST_ENABLED=true`, to the internal `COLLABORATION_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL` only (mounted as a versioned secret): last week's funnel against the §7.11 targets (`GROWTH_TARGET_STAGE`, default Gate A), evidence snapshots, stuck runs, failed demos by reason, credit holds older than 24 hours (empty until L-11's holds exist), low-balance accounts and new sign-ups to call (company, email domain, source, stage). One send per week (database claim, retry after 15 minutes, Resend idempotency key per week). Manual run: `services/evals-worker/ops-digest.ts --dry-run | --force`.
- **Growth view** (`/ops/growth`): new accounts show company, email domain, connected system, stage, last activity, latest score and credits bought (`—` until a purchase exists; never an invented 0), plus "Create CRM draft". Prospect sandboxes are excluded from every count.
- **Overview**: growth KPIs for the last 7 days against targets (sign-ups, activation, paying customers, snapshots shared) and demo health (starts, success, p50, queue, model routes, failure reasons, stuck runs).
- **Clients**: plan, credits (and bought), email domain, last activity; filters All / Managed / Free / Snapshot; search includes domains.
- **Review queue**: waiting-time badges (due at 24 h, overdue at 48 h) on test sets and results, and bulk approve with one reason (one attributed decision per result).

## Not done (and why)

- **"Assign to me"** in the Review queue: needs an assignment model and multi-operator semantics (one workspace operator and two admins today). Left for a later package.
- **Direct Warmbly API push**: the app holds no Warmbly credential and the draft must never send; the CRM draft is a database record copied into Warmbly by the operator. A scoped Warmbly key and an import adapter would be a separate, authorised change.
- **System type from onboarding answers**: L-06 adds the welcome answers to `/ops/growth`; this package shows the connected system kind.
