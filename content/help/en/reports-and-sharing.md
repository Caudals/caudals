Every finished run publishes a report. **Reports** lists them with their pass rate, status and publication date.

## Download {#export}

From a report, download it as a **PDF document**, a **Word document (.docx)**, the **CSV results** (one row per test, with the expected answer and the verdict) or **CEF JSONL** for your own tools. Everyone in the workspace can download the PDF from the **Reports** list.

## Versions {#versions}

A report can have several revisions, for example after a re-grade or a review. The **Revisions** panel lists them, and owners and editors can **Publish** the one to show.

## Share a report {#share}

Owners can share a report with people outside the workspace:

1. Open the report and choose **Share**.
2. Choose who can open it: **Private link** (anyone holding it), **Named recipient** (they must sign in) or **Workspace members**.
3. Set **Expires after** in days (14 by default, 90 at most).
4. Choose the **Sections included**. Individual test results are left out unless you include them.
5. Use **Preview shared view** to check it, then **Create access**.

The link is shown once, so copy it before you close the dialog. **Access grants** lists every share with its number of views.

## Revoke a share {#revoke}

Choose **Revoke** next to the share. New views and downloads are refused at once. Copies that were already downloaded cannot be recalled.
