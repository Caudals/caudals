The free test on [caudals.com](https://caudals.com/en/demo) gives a quick, real signal about an assistant before you create an account.

## What it does {#how}

1. You enter the page where your chat opens, an OpenAI-compatible API endpoint, or a curl command (a POST with a JSON body, with `{{question}}` where the message goes). For an API or a curl command you also say where the right answers are, usually your website.
2. Caudals reads up to six public pages of your site and writes eight questions, each with its expected answer and a quotation from the page it comes from.
3. It asks your assistant each question and grades every answer against its source.

It usually takes a few minutes, and the page shows an estimate. You can leave your email to receive the report when it is ready.

## The report {#report}

You get the number of correct answers, where the wrong ones went wrong, and every question with your assistant's answer, the expected answer and the source. Eight questions are a signal, not a full measurement: see the [methodology](https://caudals.com/en/methodology#samples).

The report has a private link. You can copy it, or send it to a colleague by email. Anyone with the link can open the report until it is deleted.

## Privacy and limits {#privacy}

- No account is needed, and the report is deleted seven days after the test.
- An API key or curl headers are used for this test only and never stored.
- Each visitor gets one free test a day.
- The test asks only ordinary customer questions.

## From the test to a workspace {#workspace}

From the report, choose **Create free account** and enter your work email (and, if you like, your company). Your new free workspace starts with the same system and your website as reference material, ready for a [full test set](help:prepare-and-run) of up to 50 questions. The eight questions of the free test stay in the free test.

You can also create a free workspace directly at app.caudals.com with a work email, or book a readout with us from the report.
