When a run finishes, the evaluation shows a summary, and the full report opens with **See every answer**.

## The score {#score}

- **N of M answers correct**: M counts the scored answers only.
- **Strict pass rate**: the same count as a percentage.
- **Quality score**: also gives half a point to each partly correct answer.
- **95% interval**: the range the pass rate would probably fall in with more questions of the same kind. The fewer the questions, the wider it is.

## The labels {#labels}

- **Correct**: conveys every key fact and contradicts nothing.
- **Partly correct**: some key facts, none contradicted.
- **Incorrect**: wrong or contradicting information, or key facts left out.
- **No answer**: the assistant deflected, asked to rephrase or replied off topic.

**Not scored** results never lower the score: **Test needs review** (the question or its expected answer may be wrong), **Not captured** (the reply could not be read reliably) and **Not run** (a timeout or error).

Answers are graded on meaning, not wording. Extra details your material does not mention are not penalised; only contradictions and missing key facts are.

## Badges {#badges}

- **Preliminary**: graded automatically. **Reviewed**: a person at Caudals has checked the results.
- **Incomplete**: fewer than 90% of the tests could be scored, or a critical test could not be assessed. Read the limitations before using the score.
- **Partial**: the run finished before every question was answered.

## The report {#report}

The report has up to five tabs: **Overview**, **Findings**, **Test results**, **Improvements** and **Methodology**.

Each finding groups failed answers by what went wrong, with what was observed, a recommended action and its evidence. **Likely causes** (invented answer, outdated document, wrong source, knowledge gap, out of scope) are hypotheses: Caudals sees the answers and your material, not your system's inner workings.

Open any result to see the question, your system's answer, the expected answer, each key fact marked included, missing or contradicted, and the excerpt from your documentation. Use the arrow keys to move between results and Esc to go back.

## Grading again {#regrade}

If your material or the grader changed, owners and editors can choose **Re-grade answers**. Caudals grades the same answers again without contacting your system, keeps every decision a person made, and publishes a new version of the report.

For the full method, see the [methodology](https://caudals.com/en/methodology).
