## Roles {#roles}

- **Viewer**: reads evaluations, results and reports, and downloads reports as PDF.
- **Editor**: everything a viewer can do, plus preparing and approving test sets, running, pausing and restarting evaluations, re-grading and publishing report versions.
- **Owner**: everything an editor can do, plus inviting people, sharing reports outside the workspace, connecting website chats in the live browser, managing schedules, API tokens and webhooks, renaming the workspace and deleting it.

## Invite a colleague {#invite}

Owners can invite people from **Settings → Members**:

1. Choose **Create invitation**.
2. Enter their email address and choose a **Workspace role** (Viewer by default).
3. Copy the private invitation link. It is shown once and expires after 24 hours. Caudals also emails the invitation, and the dialog says when it has.

The invitation can only be accepted by the email address it was sent to. You can cancel a pending invitation with **Revoke invitation**.

## Colleagues who sign up on their own {#same-company}

Each company email domain gets one self-serve workspace. If a colleague tries to create another, they are asked to request an invitation from your workspace's owner instead.

## Changing roles {#changes}

Changing a member's role is done by Caudals: write to hello@caudals.com from an owner's address. A workspace always keeps at least one owner. Removing a member is not available in the app yet; write to us if someone should lose access.
