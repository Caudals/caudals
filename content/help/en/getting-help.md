## Help in the app {#in-app}

- Open **Help** from the account menu, or press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows) and choose **Help**. Search the articles by keyword.
- The **?** next to a step opens the article about that step.
- Every article also has its own page, which you can share with a colleague in your workspace.

## Talk to us {#contact}

- Write to hello@caudals.com, or choose **Contact Caudals** in the account menu.
- For a problem with one evaluation, choose **Request operator assistance** in that evaluation's three-dot menu. It opens an email with the evaluation's name, so we know where to look.
- Book a 30-minute call from the help panel.

Tell us what you expected and what happened. Never send passwords, API keys or tokens by email: credentials belong in the app, where they are stored encrypted.

## Keep track of your work {#notifications}

The bell in the top bar lists what finished, failed or needs you (**Notifications**) and what is running now (**Activity**). Choose which notifications you also receive by email in **Settings → Notifications**.

## How we grade {#method}

The [methodology](https://caudals.com/en/methodology) explains how questions are written, how answers are graded, what each label means and what the numbers can and cannot tell you.
