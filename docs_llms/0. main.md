# Introduction

I’m a software engineer. Together with two colleagues, we’re developing a startup, which I’ll describe below.

The core idea centers on the current need for large, rich, and comprehensive datasets to train models, driven by the recent surge in artificial intelligence. We want a platform where companies, universities, labs, and researchers can post dataset requests describing their target dataset, the samples they need, the procedures contributors must follow to create the dataset, the required hardware/software or other tools, the protocol to follow for data collection, and more. From there, users can sign up on our site and contribute to building the various datasets that are posted.

Our initial business model is as follows—though you’re welcome to propose new ideas or improvements. Organizations that need datasets pay a fee to each contributor based on the work required for data collection. Our platform takes a commission for each contributor who participates in a dataset, or alternatively charges a one-time fee for posting the request on the platform.

Some optional features for our startup could include:

- Open-source datasets where users contribute altruistically.
- An open repository hosting the open-source datasets.
- Partnerships with universities, research centers, etc.
- The ability to contribute to datasets in exchange for perks (e.g., free subscriptions to a platform for a period of time, AI platform credits/tokens, complimentary services, etc.)
