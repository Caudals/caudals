The buyer side

AI learns by looking at examples: without good examples, there is no good AI. And good examples are not easy to come by.

The question today isn't which model we train. The question is what we train it on.

Too generic
Public data doesn't fit the sector, geography, or format the buyer actually needs.
Too ambiguous
Usage rights are unclear. Legal won't approve training on it.
Too slow
Internal teams and labelers can't source, license, and deliver end to end.
